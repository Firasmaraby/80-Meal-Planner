# Caribbean Kitchen: draft week (names and one-line ideas)

Written before any grams, then revised after the lab. This is the final week.

Places: Jamaica (ackee and saltfish, callaloo and egg, jerk chicken, rice and peas, run down mackerel, escovitch snapper, peanut punch), Trinidad and Tobago (curry channa and aloo, curry goat, pineapple and cucumber chow), Cuba (black bean soup, sofrito eggs with warm milk), Puerto Rico (shrimp guisado with recaito and culantro) and Haiti (griot with pikliz).

Carbs: brown rice, whole-wheat flour and bread, yuca, green plantain, pumpkin, beans and chickpeas. No white rice, so the week is "whole grains too". Plantain is baked, never fried.

Fish: run down mackerel on Wednesday (leftover Thursday lunch) and canned sardines on whole-wheat toast on Thursday and Sunday, so there is a fatty fish day well apart from Wednesday. Escovitch snapper on Sunday is the lean white fish, pan-seared rather than fried. Shrimp on Monday, salt cod in the ackee breakfasts.

## Breakfasts (3 recipes)
- Ackee and saltfish with callaloo, boiled green plantain and avocado. Mon, Thu, Sun.
- Cuban-style sofrito scrambled eggs on whole-wheat toast with warm milk. Tue, Sat.
- Jamaican callaloo and egg scramble on whole-wheat toast with avocado. Wed, Fri.

## Dinners and the lunches they make
1. Mon: Jerk chicken with brown rice and peas and a lime slaw. Makes 2, Tuesday lunch.
2. Tue: Trinidad curry channa and aloo on whole-wheat flatbread (doubles-inspired), with callaloo folded in. Makes 2, Wednesday lunch.
3. Wed: Run down mackerel with pumpkin and whole-wheat dumplings. Makes 2, Thursday lunch.
4. Thu: Cuban black bean soup with a little pork, avocado and pickled onion. Makes 2, Friday lunch.
5. Fri: Haitian griot-style pork, simmered then roasted, with pikliz and baked cassava. Makes 2, Saturday lunch.
6. Sat (weekend project): Trinidad curry goat with pumpkin and a green seasoning, brown rice and cucumber chow. Makes 2, Sunday lunch.
7. Sun: Escovitch snapper with baked plantain and a lime slaw. 1 serving.

## Lunches
- Mon: Puerto Rican-style stewed shrimp with recaito and boiled yuca.
- Tue to Sun: leftovers.

## Snacks (2 a day, Light drops the second)
Peanuts with lime and pepper, banana milk, pumpkin wedges, cucumber chow, mango with lime, peanut punch (evaporated milk, peanuts and banana), pineapple chow, sardines on toast (Thursday and Sunday). Big adds one a day: banana with peanuts (twice), roasted channa (twice), baked plantain chips, peanuts with lime, avocado toast.

## Checks against GUIDE section 11
- Ingredients not of the cuisine: none added for a number. Evaporated milk is used in Caribbean porridge and punch, peanuts are the Caribbean nut, and callaloo, culantro and Scotch bonnet are the real thing.
- Dishes renamed: doubles-inspired, griot (baked), escovitch (pan-seared), run down (fresh mackerel), camarones guisados (with yuca). Each says "inspired" where we changed the method.
- Breakfasts: ackee and saltfish, callaloo with egg and Cuban eggs with toast are everyday Caribbean breakfasts.
- Same flavour base twice in a day: the Cuban sofrito breakfasts (Tue, Sat) never fall on the Cuban soup days (Thu dinner, Fri lunch). Monday's Puerto Rican shrimp lunch uses recaito and culantro, not the thyme and allspice of the ackee and the jerk.
- One vegetable everywhere: plantain only in ackee and escovitch, cassava only Mon lunch and Fri, pumpkin in the mackerel and goat dishes, callaloo is the green and cabbage, cucumber, carrot rotate.
- Eggs: 4 slots, the Cuban eggs (Tue, Sat) and the callaloo scramble (Wed, Fri). No egg snacks and no eggs on salads or in the soup. Choline comes from the eggs, pork, goat, fish, shrimp and beans.
- Callaloo: ackee (3), scramble (2) and the Trinidad channa bhaji (1 dish, 2 slots). Not in the shrimp, run down or goat, where it did not belong.
- Avocado: 9 slots at Standard, mostly breakfasts. Taking it out of the ackee breakfast broke the Light 1400 and fibre gates.
- Dairy: 6 slots, evaporated milk in the warm milk with the eggs, banana milk and peanut punch for calcium.
- Nuts: peanuts only, 3 slots at Standard, 6 at Big.
- Missing: green seasoning, sofrito, recaito and jerk paste are made in the recipe from fresh herbs; "West Indian style" curry powder is plain curry powder.
