# keto-latin draft: Keto Latin (final week)

Latin American home cooking with the starch taken out and the salsas, avocado and fish left in. Places: Mexico (huevos a la mexicana, salsa verde eggs, carne asada, camarones a la diabla, achiote chicken, nopales), Peru (sea bass ceviche, trout in ají amarillo, palta rellena), Brazil (moqueca with couve and a pecan farofa), Argentina (steak with chimichurri and a green salad).

Fat comes from avocado, olive oil, pecans, almonds, pepitas, chia and fish. There is no cheese or cream, and the coconut milk is light and used once. Eye of round, pork tenderloin, chicken breast, sea bass and shrimp are the lean proteins, because olive oil, avocado and eggs already push saturated fat toward 10%. Standard is written near 1,900 kcal and the lab sizes it up; nothing at Big grows past about 375 g of raw meat or fish.

## Check against GUIDE section 11

- **Breakfasts:** three recipes. Chia pudding on four days (Mon, Wed, Sun, plus a Mexican chocolate version on Sat), huevos a la mexicana once, salsa verde eggs twice. Egg slots: 4 of 35 (3 breakfasts and the palta rellena, which is a Peruvian classic with egg).
- **Dairy:** zero slots. No queso or crema on purpose, to keep saturated fat under 10%. The coconut milk is the light kind, 35 g, in the moqueca only.
- **Nuts and seeds:** about 21 of 35 slots, because pecans, almonds, pepitas and chia are the plant fat that keeps saturated fat low. Each one is used where the cuisine does: pepitas as the cancha-style crunch and in sauces, pecans from Northern Mexico (and in place of walnuts in the ají sauce and cassava farofa), chia from chía fresca.
- **Same flavour base twice in a day:** tomato, lime and cilantro run through most Mexican and Peruvian dishes, so each day has one raw salsa or ceviche-style dish and one cooked, smoky or herby one (ancho, achiote, guajillo and chipotle, ají amarillo, chimichurri, colorau).
- **One vegetable everywhere:** avocado is the fat, so it shows up often, but in different forms (guacamole, sliced, in ceviche, in palta rellena). Greens rotate through spinach, collards, romaine, arugula, nopales, chayote, zucchini and cabbage.
- **Lists match steps:** raw weights for every meat, fish and shellfish. Tuna and sardines are canned, and the egg in the palta rellena is boiled in the steps.
- **Weekday time:** the 15-minute tag is only on recipes with 15 minutes of prep plus cook or less. The tomatillo and nopales breakfasts take 22 to 28 minutes, with make-ahead tips.
- **Recipes that need other recipes:** guacamole, salsas, pickled onion, chimichurri and farofa are all made inside the dish that uses them.
- **Cooking sense:** lean beef is cooked to medium, chicken breast and pork tenderloin are not overcooked, raw fish is bought for ceviche, and every dish with raw weights uses what you buy.

## Check against GUIDE section 12 (Keto)

- **Net carbs:** written Standard days run about 26 to 33 g net, with the lab's worst day at 39 g at 2,800 kcal. There are no grains, potatoes, sugar, beans or fruit except lime, avocado and a few spoonfuls of vegetable.
- **Saturated fat:** under 10% every day at every edge. The tightest is 9.96% at Light 1,800 and 9.78% at Big 2,400. Olive oil is capped around 7 to 15 g a dish.
- **Fixing the usual keto gaps:** thiamin (pork tenderloin, chia, pecans), folate (spinach, collards, avocado, tomatillo), vitamin C (peppers, tomatillo, lime, spinach), potassium (avocado, spinach, nopales), magnesium (pepitas, chia, almonds), calcium (chia, sardines, nopales, collards, fortified almond milk), fibre (chia, avocado, nopales), choline (eggs, beef, fish).
- **Omega-6 to omega-3:** weekly about 2.3 to 3.8, worst day under 9. Trout and sardines on non-adjacent days, plus chia on four breakfasts and two chía fresca snacks.
- **Critic round:** thin-cut pounded eye of round for both steaks (sirloin would add a grocery), a thicker trout and diabla sauce, a wetter moqueca, a dressed pork slaw, zucchini in place of chayote, cocoa in the Saturday pudding, a drinkable chía fresca, and egg notes left blank so counts render at every size. Meat was not cut further because choline on day 1 fails below the written amounts.
- **Big band:** 2,800 to 3,200 is dropped. At 3,200 the lab pushed net carbs past 40 g, meat past 400 g in a serving, and zinc past its upper limit, so Big claims 2,400 to 2,800 only.
