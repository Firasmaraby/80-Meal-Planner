# North Indian & Pakistani Home Kitchen: draft week (names and one-line ideas)

Written before any grams, then revised after every lab run and after the chef critic's review. This is the revised week.

Places: Punjab (dal palak tadka, saag paneer, methi besan chilla, methi missi paratha, mackerel curry, khichdi), Delhi (chicken chaat, rajma, tandoori fish tray, bhurji), Lahore (chicken karahi, nihari, fruit chaat), Lucknow (nihari, which is also Old Delhi's).

Carbs: whole-wheat atta roti and parathas, brown basmati, cracked wheat (daliya), lentils, chickpeas and kidney beans. No white rice or naan, so the week is "whole grains too". Brown basmati on 4 days (Mon, Tue, Wed, Sat), roti on the rest.

Fish: Friday (Atlantic mackerel curry) and Sunday (tandoori-spiced salmon tray). Two days apart, never a leftover.

## Breakfasts (4 recipes)
- Vegetable daliya with masoor dal, green peas and a boiled egg. Mon, Thu. Cook the night before if you like.
- Egg besan chilla with fresh methi, ajwain and a peanut-green chutney. Tue, Fri. About 13 minutes.
- Egg and paneer bhurji with spinach and a hot roti. Wed, Sun. Knead the dough the night before.
- Methi missi paratha with boiled eggs and dahi. Saturday, the slow weekend breakfast.

## Dinners and the lunches they make
1. Mon: Dal palak tadka (masoor) with brown basmati and cucumber raita. 2 servings, makes Tuesday's lunch.
2. Tue: Rajma masala with brown basmati. 2 servings, makes Wednesday's lunch.
3. Wed: Chicken karahi with roti (Lahore). 2 servings, makes Thursday's lunch.
4. Thu: Sarson da saag with paneer, roti and a glass of salted lassi. 2 servings, makes Friday's lunch.
5. Fri: Mackerel curry with mustard seed, ginger and tomato, with roti. 1 serving.
6. Sat (weekend project): Nihari-inspired goat stew with small rotis, ginger and lemon. 2 servings, makes Sunday's lunch.
7. Sun: Tandoori-spiced salmon with roasted cauliflower and okra, mint raita and roti. 1 serving.

## Lunches
- Mon: Chicken tikka and chana chaat with a crisp-chickpea topping.
- Sat: Masoor dal khichdi with cauliflower and a ghee tadka.
- Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sun: leftovers.

## Snacks (2 a day, Light drops the second)
Haldi doodh with almonds, masala chaas with almonds, pomegranate raita with almonds, guava lassi, masala roasted chickpeas, peanut chaat with kachumber, pomegranate and almonds, apple chaat, fruit chaat, guava with chaat masala. Big adds a third snack each day.

## Check against GUIDE section 11
- Cuisine fit: nothing added just for a number. The new spices (hing, ajwain, amchur, chaat masala, Kashmiri chili, brown mustard seeds, bay leaf) are the North Indian pantry. No flax, no walnuts, no mustard oil.
- Honest names: "nihari-inspired" (a faster stew), "tandoori-spiced" (an oven tray), "chicken tikka" only because the chicken is marinated, "mackerel curry" inspired by Punjabi machhi curry and Karachi machli salan.
- Breakfasts are four different plates, not one egg dish in three costumes.
- One flavour base a day: cumin tadka (dal), green chutney (chilla), tomato and black pepper (karahi), mustard seed and onion (fish), caramelized onion and coriander (nihari).
- Dahi is cut by about half from the first draft and is a real part of the dish: raita, lassi, chaas, a spoon beside rajma and khichdi. Low-fat Greek yogurt everywhere.
- Raw weights for chicken, goat, fish, dal, rice, atta, paneer and eggs. Canned chickpeas and kidney beans only.
- Raitas, chutneys and the lassi are written into the recipe that uses them.

## What changed after the critic
- Mustard oil, flax and walnuts are gone. Canola is the cooking oil, ghee finishes the dal, khichdi and saag tadkas and brushes the paratha.
- Guava appears three times only: guava lassi, guava with chaat masala and the fruit chaat.
- Rice is on 4 days. The rajma is now a Tuesday dinner that makes Wednesday's lunch.
- The chana palak is replaced by a salmon tray with a palak raita, so rajma is the only legume curry on rice.
- Eggs: a single boiled egg in the daliya is needed to keep choline above the line for men on the days with no other egg. Mornings with 2 or more eggs: Tue, Wed, Fri, Sat, Sun.
- Groceries: exactly 45, the limit. To add the new spices, mint, sweet potato, cornmeal, kasuri methi, fennel, ground ginger and cardamom had to go.

## After the pantry rule change
- Spices no longer count toward the 45 groceries (38 fresh groceries now, a spice shelf of about 22). The flavour came back: the nihari set (green cardamom, cinnamon, cloves, dry ginger, bay leaf, Kashmiri chili, black pepper), kasuri methi in the dal, saag and bhurji, kala namak in the chaats, whole dried red chili in the dal, khichdi and saag tadkas, cornmeal in the saag, and mint in the chaat chutney, the chaas and the raita.
- Spinach repetition cut from 8 recipes to 3 (dal, khichdi, bhurji). Fresh methi is in the chilla, the daliya and the missi paratha, mustard greens carry the saag on their own, and okra joins the salmon tray as the dry vegetable.
- Dal tadka lost its ginger and the nihari lost its garlic, because of the 14-ingredient cap. Tamarind paste stayed out of the chaat (lemon and chaat masala do the sour). Coriander powder is not in the food library, so crushed coriander seed stands in.
- Eggs stay on every morning. Without the single boiled egg in the daliya, choline falls to 56% on Monday and Thursday for men, and nothing else in those days (milk, paneer, more chicken) closes that gap.

## Final review fixes
- Dal: lemon in the list and in the tadka step (kasuri methi stays in the saag and bhurji). Fruit chaat: orange instead of pomegranate. Kachumber is now a peanut chaat. Tikka marinade has Kashmiri chili and chaat masala. Mackerel curry retitled. Daliya uses green peas, not methi, and has no stray pepper. Haldi doodh and the pomegranate raita swap between Day 2 and Day 3. Roti dough is step 1 in the karahi, saag and salmon recipes. Nihari trades the bay leaf for garlic. Text fixes in the chilla, karahi, saag, howItWorks and a few lists.
