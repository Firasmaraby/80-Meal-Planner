# Gluten-free Latin Kitchen: draft week (names and one-line ideas)

Written before any grams, revised after the lab. This is the final week.

Latin American home cooking is gluten-free by its nature (corn, rice, beans, potatoes, cassava, plantain, quinoa), so nothing here stands in for anything else. The only risks are hidden ones (stock cubes, sausages, achiote, colorau, chipotle in adobo and chile blends), so the week uses no sausage, no stock cubes and no oats, and the tips say "check the label" where a product could carry wheat.

Places: Colombia (arepas with huevos pericos, hogao beef with maduros, ajiaco), Mexico (caldo tlalpeño, picadillo tacos, pork tamales, chía fresca), Peru (quinoa soup, salmon in ají amarillo, causa, quinua con leche), Brazil (tapioca with queijo coalho, rice and beans with couve, galinhada, moqueca capixaba).

Starches rotate on purpose: masarepa and masa (corn), rice, quinoa, potato, cassava, plantain and tapioca. Tortillas show up once (picadillo tacos) plus a few wedges with the guacamole.

Fish: salmon in ají amarillo on Tuesday, sardine causa on Thursday, salmon moqueca on Friday, plus a sardine snack on Sunday. The two salmon dinners are three days apart and every fish dish is served fresh.

Omega-3 on the days without fish: ground flaxseed stirred into the yogurt that goes with the Brazilian tapioca crepes and into the banana yogurt (iogurte com linhaça), and chía fresca (a Mexican drink) on two days. The quinoa porridge has no seeds. No nuts anywhere.

## Breakfasts (3 recipes)
- Arepas with huevos pericos, spinach and black beans (Colombia). Mon, Wed, Sun.
- Tapioca crepes with queijo coalho, a fried egg and linhaça mango yogurt (Brazil). Tue, Sat.
- Quinoa con leche with banana, cinnamon and clove (Peru), cooked as a double batch. Thu, with the second bowl on Fri.

2. Tue: Roasted salmon in ají amarillo glaze with crisp potatoes and salsa criolla. 1 serving.
3. Wed: Beef picadillo tacos with carrot, peas and cabbage slaw. 1 serving.
4. Thu: Galinhada with chicken, a little raw chicken liver stirred into the tomato, corn, peas and spinach. Makes 2, so Friday lunch.
5. Fri: Salmon moqueca capixaba-style with mashed cassava and garlicky couve. 1 serving.
6. Sat (weekend project): Pork tamales in red chile salsa with lime cabbage slaw. Makes 2, so Sunday lunch (about 2 hours start to finish).
7. Sun: Colombian ajiaco with chicken, corn and potatoes. 1 serving.

## Snacks (2 a day, Light drops the second)
Chía fresca (Mon, Wed); roasted chickpeas (Mon, Thu); black bean dip with carrot and red pepper (Tue, Thu); orange with lime and chile (Tue, Sat); guacamole with red pepper and baked tortilla wedges (Wed, Sat); banana yogurt with flaxseed and mango with lime and chile (Fri); sardines with lime, tomato and onion and grilled queijo coalho with orange (Sun).

Big adds a third snack: chicken anticuchos, sardines with lime and tomato, black bean and corn cup, hard-boiled eggs with chile, mango. All are protein-led and low in glycaemic load, because the starchy snacks I tried first pushed Big over the limits.

## Checks against GUIDE sections 11 and 12
- Gluten: no wheat, barley, rye, spelt, bulgur, couscous, farro, semolina or seitan, no soy sauce, no oats. Water and the meat make the broths. The label warnings are kept for the real risks (tortillas, masarepa, masa harina, tapioca starch, colorau, chipotle in adobo, ají paste, boxed broth, bulk-bin quinoa, sardines in sauce).
- Not of the cuisine: flax is used where Brazilians use linhaça (tapioca, yogurt), chia where Mexicans use it (chía fresca) and in sweet porridge. No walnuts or almonds dropped into savoury dishes. Chicken liver is a small, real addition to galinhada.
- Renamed dishes: the moqueca, tamales, ajiaco, causa, picadillo and galinhada say "inspired" where the fish, the fat or the method changed.
- Same flavour base twice in a day: ají amarillo is Tuesday dinner, Thursday lunch (causa) and nothing else; chipotle is Wednesday lunch only; hogao is Saturday lunch only.
- One vegetable everywhere: carrot is in four recipes, red pepper in five, avocado in five (never more than twice in a day), orange in four (never more than twice in a day), mango in two (never twice in a day).
- Crutches counted at Standard: eggs in 6 slots (the five arepa and tapioca breakfasts plus the causa; the choline gate forced them), dairy in 9 slots (coalho and yogurt where the dish already has them, milk in the porridge), nuts in 0, fatty fish in 4, chia or flax in 5.
- Lists match steps: raw chicken, liver, pork, beef, salmon, cassava, potato and plantain; dried beans and rice cooked ahead with canned equivalents given in the steps.
- Dried corn husks for the tamales are a wrapper, not a food, so they are not in the ingredient list (no matching food exists).
