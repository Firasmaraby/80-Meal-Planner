# Diabetes-friendly Middle Eastern: draft week (names and one-line ideas)

Written after the family file and revised after every lab run. This is the final week.

Places: Turkey (kahvaltı plate, mercimek çorbası), Lebanon (hummus with shawarma chicken, fattoush with sardines, kofta with tabbouleh), Syria and Aleppo (mujaddara, muhammara, freekeh with chicken, chickpea fatteh), Palestine (maqluba), Egypt and the Levant (ful medames), the Gulf (mackerel in loomi and tomato).

Carbs: one small, honest starch in each of the three meals, always next to beans, lentils, eggs, chicken, fish or yogurt. Pita is a few bites or a handful of crunch, bulgur and freekeh are a few spoonfuls, brown basmati is a thin layer under the maqluba. No white rice, no juice, no sugar. Carb style: everything in moderation by the lab's label (whole-wheat pita, bulgur, freekeh and brown basmati are all whole grains).

Fish: Tuesday (fresh sardines), Friday (salmon) and Sunday (mackerel), plus canned sardines on pita as Thursday and Saturday snacks. Tuesday, Friday and Sunday are never adjacent, always cooked fresh, never a leftover.

## Breakfasts (3 recipes)
- Turkish-style breakfast plate: two boiled eggs, white cheese, olives, walnuts, tomato, cucumber, rocket and a wedge of pita. Mon, Wed, Sat. Eggs boiled on Sunday.
- Ful medames with chickpeas, lemon, cumin and tomato. Thu, Fri, Sun. Ten minutes from canned.
- Shakshuka with chickpeas, feta and a small pita. Tue only. Sauce made on Monday evening.

## Dinners and the lunches they make
1. Mon: Mujaddara with lentils and a little bulgur, lemony cabbage salad, garlicky yogurt. 2 servings, Tuesday's lunch.
2. Tue: Grilled sardines with fattoush. 1 serving, fresh.
3. Wed: Freekeh bil djej, chicken and cracked freekeh with chickpeas and almonds, arugula salad. 2 servings, Thursday's lunch.
4. Thu: Lamb kofta with parsley tabbouleh and walnut tarator. 2 servings, Friday's lunch.
5. Fri: Samke harra-style salmon on roasted cauliflower and an arugula salad, with a tahini drizzle. 1 serving, fresh.
6. Sat (weekend project): Maqluba with lamb, eggplant and cauliflower and a little brown basmati. 2 servings, Sunday's lunch.
7. Sun: Gulf-style mackerel in loomi and tomato sauce with spinach and a little bulgur. 1 serving, fresh.

## Lunches cooked fresh
- Mon: Hummus with shawarma-spiced chicken and a carrot and parsley salad.
- Wed: Chickpea fatteh with garlicky yogurt and toasted pita.
- Sat: Mercimek çorbası with a small pita and an arugula salad.
- Tue, Thu, Fri, Sun: leftovers.

## Snacks (2 a day, Light keeps the first, Big adds a third)
Muhammara with pepper and carrot sticks (Monday only), lupini beans with lemon and mint, apple with almonds or walnuts, oven-roasted chickpeas with sumac, sardines on pita, pomegranate with walnuts, labneh with cucumber and za'atar, hummus with vegetables. Big adds warm chickpeas (Mon), labneh and za'atar on pita (Tue, Sat), cacik with walnuts (Wed, Thu) and hummus with pita and vegetables (Fri, Sun).

## Light size
Light claims 1400-1600 and 1600-1800 only. I tried 1200-1400 and the lab refused it: the days will not size below about 1,230 to 1,370 kcal without losing the nutrients women need, so the week cannot honestly claim it. At 1,400 the pita comes down to 13 to 25 g a meal (about a mini pita half, a few bites), the bulgur to 11 to 14 g dry and the brown basmati to 14 g dry, which the tips call "a spoonful or two" and "a few spoonfuls". Nothing is shrunk to a crumb, and the vegetables and legumes stay as written.

## Check against GUIDE sections 11 and 12
- Cuisine fit: every dish is something a home cook in the region would put on the table. The tweaks are proportion (more legumes and vegetables, less starch), never a different dish. Names are honest: "Turkish-style", "Gulf-style mackerel" (Atlantic mackerel in place of kanaad), "samke harra-style salmon", "Freekeh bil djej", and the muhammara is the Aleppo style.
- Carbs are spread: each main meal has one starch with protein and fibre beside it. Fruit always comes with nuts or yogurt.
- Legumes at two meals on most days: ful, hummus, fatteh, mujaddara, lentil soup, shakshuka, freekeh, maqluba and the salmon's chickpea salad.
- Acid and vegetables first: a salad or raw vegetables sit beside every dinner, with lemon, sumac or pomegranate molasses.
- No juice, no honey, no dried fruit. The only sweet is a teaspoon of pomegranate molasses in the dressings and the muhammara.
- Weeknight honesty: mujaddara (Mon) takes about 40 minutes if the onions are browned on Sunday, and freekeh (Wed) is mostly hands-off. The shakshuka sauce is made the evening before. Only maqluba (Sat) is a project dish, with a make-ahead tip.
- Flavour bases: the hummus snack moved from Friday (a ful, tahini and chickpea day) to Saturday, so no day has the same chickpea and tahini base twice in the snacks and the breakfast. Muhammara is now one slot (Monday's snack). The kofta takes a walnut and tahini tarator, which keeps the walnuts and omega-3 on Thursday.
- Crutch counts (Standard): eggs 4 slots (three kahvaltı plates and the shakshuka); dairy 11 slots (feta in four meals, yogurt in five, labneh twice); chickpeas 12 slots (down from 13); nuts 16 slots, mostly walnuts where the cuisine uses them (kahvaltı plate, muhammara, tarator, pomegranate with walnuts) and almonds on the freekeh and maqluba; fatty fish 5 slots (three fresh, two canned sardine snacks).
- Fat is olive oil throughout. No flax, chia, canola or butter.

## What changed after the lab and the read-through
- Light 1200-1400 does not pass, so Light claims 1400-1800.
- The hummus snack moved from Friday to Saturday, and Friday's snack is labneh with cucumber and za'atar, so Friday no longer stacks chickpeas and tahini three times. Saturday's apple and walnuts snack gave way to it, and Big's Saturday add is labneh on pita so hummus is not twice that day.
- Sunday's muhammara snack became pomegranate with walnuts, to cut the red pepper count and the muhammara repeats.
- Chef critic fixes: salmon salad loses its chickpeas (roasted cauliflower, a sumac onion and arugula salad, a taratour-style tahini drizzle); kofta swaps muhammara for a walnut and tahini tarator; baharat comes out of the mujaddara, freekeh (cardamom and bay instead), kofta and mackerel; the sardine pita swaps tomato for red onion and sumac; maqluba is packed tighter and rests 15 minutes; shawarma chicken is marinated and rested; the muhammara tip says to make four portions; the mackerel sits skin side down on the bulgur; fattoush and fatteh pita is toasted in a dry pan; the soup gets a spoonful of passata; the shakshuka loses its splash of water; roasted chickpeas are eaten the same day.
- After the lab: day 4 lost vitamin A and omega-3 with the muhammara, so the freekeh pot gained 100 g carrot and 12 g almonds and the tarator carries 10 g walnuts. Friday's Big snack is hummus with pita (the Standard hummus snack is on Saturday), so Big Friday is not two dairy snacks.
- Tips fixed: the hummus and hummus snack tips no longer promise snacks that are not in the week; the mujaddara tip says to brown the onions on Sunday; the freekeh tip says the cooking is hands-off.
- The mackerel sauce takes about half a cup of water, not a full cup, so the bulgur and fish do not stew.
- `howItWorks` prep paragraph now matches the week: eggs for Monday's and Wednesday's plates on Sunday, onions for the mujaddara on Sunday, shakshuka sauce on Monday evening.
- Final review: Friday's second snack is apple with almonds (walnuts were at three Friday meals), and Big Wednesday adds cacik instead of a third chickpea snack. The maqluba broth now only reaches the top of the thin rice layer in a small deep pot. The kofta tarator is chopped and stirred, not blitzed. Mackerel, salmon, hummus, fatteh, muhammara, freekeh, shakshuka and mercimek wording and timings were corrected, and the roasted chickpeas cook time is 35 minutes.
