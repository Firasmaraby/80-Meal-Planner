# Vegan Indian: draft week (names and one-line ideas)

Places: Punjab (rajma, palak tofu, chaat, raita), Gujarat (kadhi khichdi with a cauliflower and carrot shaak), Maharashtra (kanda poha with chai, soya keema matar, koshimbir), Tamil Nadu (idli, sambar, idli podi) and Kerala (tofu roast in the nadan style with a cabbage thoran, mushroom and soya chunk pepper fry, achari soya bites), plus a Hyderabadi dum biryani for the weekend.

Carbs: dals and beans first, then brown basmati and whole-wheat atta. Thick poha and parboiled idli rice are the only white grains. Almonds (not cashews) are the nut in the rajma, palak, haldi doodh, kheer and biryani, and carry the vitamin E. Labelled "everything in moderation" because of them.

Fortified B12: every day has a glass or more of fortified soy beverage: in the poha chai (Tue, Thu), the masala chai (Mon, Sun), haldi doodh (Wed, Thu), mango lassi (Fri) and kheer (Sat). Plain soy yogurt is never counted for B12. `howItWorks` says the numbers assume about 2 µg of B12 per cup (the library's soy beverage), that many Canadian cartons carry about half that, and that a daily B12 supplement is the safe bet.

## Breakfasts (3 recipes)
- Moong and besan chilla with spinach and a sharp mint chutney with ground flax (no yogurt). Mon, Wed, Fri.
- Kanda poha (thick poha) with peanuts and peas, with a cup of soy masala chai. Tue, Thu.
- Idli with sambar and flax-peanut chutney powder (weekend project, batter started Friday afternoon). Sat, Sun (leftover idlis and sambar).

## Dinners and the lunches they make
1. Mon: Rajma masala with brown basmati and cucumber raita. Makes Tuesday's lunch.
2. Tue: Palak tofu with whole-wheat roti (calcium-set tofu stands in for paneer). Makes Wednesday's lunch.
3. Wed: Gujarati soy-yogurt kadhi with moong khichdi and cauliflower-carrot shaak. Makes Thursday's lunch.
4. Thu: Soya, moong and mushroom keema matar with whole-wheat roti and lemon. Makes Friday's lunch.
5. Fri: Kerala-style tofu roast with carrot and cabbage thoran and brown rice. Makes Saturday's lunch.
6. Sat (weekend project): Soya chunk and mushroom dum biryani with mint raita. Makes Sunday's lunch.
7. Sun: Dal tadka with spinach (toor and moong), whole-wheat roti and gobi matar (cauliflower, peas and mushrooms). One serving.

## Lunches

- Tue to Sun: leftovers.

## Snacks (1 or 2 a day)
- Mon: soy masala chai; achari soya chunk bites.
- Tue: mushroom and soya chunk pepper fry; soya tikki with mint chutney.
- Wed: haldi doodh with almonds; guava and mango fruit chaat with walnuts.
- Thu: moong kosambari (with roasted peanuts); haldi doodh with almonds.
- Fri: mango soy lassi with almonds; cucumber and peanut koshimbir.
- Sat: brown rice kheer with soy beverage and almonds; soya tikki with mint chutney.
- Sun: soy masala chai; masala roasted chana.
- Big adds a snack each day: peanut sundal (Mon), moong kosambari (Tue), mushroom and soya chunk pepper fry (Wed), masala roasted chana (Thu), til-alsi laddoo with walnuts (Fri), cucumber and peanut koshimbir (Sat), achari soya bites (Sun).
- Light (1600 to 1800) is the Standard week scaled down, dropping one snack on five days: Mon (bites), Thu (kosambari), Fri (koshimbir), Sat (tikki) and Sun (roasted chana). Rotis and rice are small portions and the tips say so.

## Check against GUIDE sections 11 and 12
- Tofu: chaat bowl, palak tofu (2 slots) and the Kerala roast (2 slots), 5 slots. Soya chunks: achari bites, pepper fry, biryani (2), 4 slots; no seitan. Soy granules: soya tikki (2) and keema (2). Legumes: rajma, chickpeas, toor, moong, urad, besan, peanuts.
- Fortified soy beverage: every day. Soy yogurt: 8 slots, at most 2 in a day. The chilla, koshimbir, achari bites, tikki, keema and dal tadka have none. No dairy, no ghee, no honey, no paneer, no mustard oil. No `about` credits soy yogurt with vitamin E or C (the library value was corrected).
- Vitamin E comes from almonds (kheer, haldi doodh, lassi, rajma, palak, biryani), peanuts, canola oil and spinach (dal palak). No seeds sprinkled for numbers.
- Flax only in the chilla chutney and the idli podi; walnuts in the fruit chaat and the Big laddoo. Canola only for cooking.
- Library staples used: thick poha, soya chunks. Kala chana and chana dal are not used.
- Written Standard days (kcal): 2066, 2133, 2079, 2276, 2074, 2316, 2249.
- 1400 to 1600 is not claimed: at that size day 2 goes over 9:1 omega and day 4 cannot be trimmed far enough without losing nutrients.
