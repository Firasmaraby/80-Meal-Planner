# Diabetes-friendly Latin: draft week (names and one-line ideas)

Written before the grams and revised after every lab run. This is the final week.

Places: Mexico (huevos rancheros, chicken tinga, pozole rojo, ceviche de camaron), Colombia (arepa with black beans and queso fresco, arroz con pollo), Peru (quinoa and chickpea salad, mackerel escabeche with camote), Cuba and the Dominican Republic (black bean soup, ropa vieja, habichuelas with garlic shrimp), El Salvador (salmon with salsa verde and curtido).

Carbs: one honest starch at each of the three meals, always beside beans, eggs, fish or meat and a pile of raw or lightly cooked vegetables: one corn tortilla, a small arepa, a few spoonfuls of brown rice or quinoa, a little camote, a few slices of baked ripe plantain, a scoop of hominy in a pot that is mostly cabbage. No juice, no sugar, no dried fruit, no honey. Beans and chickpeas come from cans (drained and rinsed) so weeknights stay realistic.

Fish: mackerel on Tuesday and salmon on Friday, never leftovers. Canned sardines (tostada, lettuce cups) on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday carry calcium, B12 and iron. Shrimp on Saturday and Sunday.

## Snacks (2 a day, Light keeps the first, Big adds a third)
Salpicon de pollo on jicama, jicama with lime and pepitas, sardine tostada, garbanzos tostados, shrimp coctel, sardine lettuce cups with ají amarillo, spiced pepitas, toasted almonds with lime and chile, guacamole with vegetables. Big adds a low-glycaemic third snack: camote, boiled egg, pepitas, garbanzos, guacamole or pico de gallo with queso fresco.

## Light size
Light claims 1600-1800 only. I tried 1200-1400 and 1400-1600 and the lab refused them: the week will not size below about 1,480 to 1,660 kcal a day without dropping iron, calcium or a B vitamin under 80% for the woman it is graded for, because beans, queso fresco, sardines and chicken are doing all of that work and the vegetables are fixed. At 1,600 the tortillas are about 15 g (a 3 to 4 inch tortilla), the arepa is 25 g and the rice is 25 to 40 g dry, so nothing is shrunk to a crumb.

## Check against GUIDE sections 11 and 12
- Cuisine: every dish is a real Latin American home dish and is named for what it is. Escabeche, ceviche, curtido and the soup say "inspired" where the method or the starch moved.
- Staples used for real: chipotle in adobo (tinga), achiote paste (arroz con pollo), ají amarillo (escabeche, quinoa dressing), guajillo (pozole rojo) and masarepa (arepa). No chili powder stands in for them; chili powder is only dusted on snacks. Culantro, ancho, guascas and colorau were left out to stay under 45 groceries; cilantro does the work.
- Carbs spread: breakfast, lunch and dinner each have one small starch with protein and fibre. Fruit comes with yogurt and nuts, never alone. Snacks are built on beans, sardines, chicken, shrimp, nuts, egg or vegetables.
- Legumes at two or three meals every day.
- Acid and fibre first: lime, vinegar and a salad, slaw or raw vegetable on every dinner plate.
- Crutch counts at Standard: eggs in 4 slots (three rancheros and the soup); dairy in 10 slots (3 yogurt breakfasts, queso fresco in rancheros, quinoa salad, soup, salmon and arepa); nuts and seeds in 6 slots (almonds and pepitas on the papaya breakfast, almonds twice, pepitas on jicama once); fatty fish in 5 slots (mackerel, salmon, 3 sardine snacks).
- Flavour bases do not repeat in a day: chipotle, ají amarillo, achiote, guajillo and tomatillo each get their own day. Cucumber is in 4 recipes, avocado in 4, carrot in 4, red pepper in 4.
- Fat is canola throughout. No flax, no chia, no olive oil.

## What changed after the lab
- Grocery count came down from 63 to 45 by cutting extra beans (lima, cannellini, kidney), a second vinegar, scallion, red onion, jalapeño, ancho, pumpkin, culantro, capers, crushed tomato, peas, peanuts, snapper and orange.
- Sat fat: queso fresco snacks went, sardines and almonds took over calcium; breakfasts were arranged so the two heaviest sat-fat days (Tuesday, Friday) start with yogurt.
- Vitamin E and iron: almonds went onto the yogurt breakfast and onto two snacks; the black bean dip moved to Friday; guajillo, cumin and oregano went up in the pozole.
- GL: Wednesday starts with yogurt, not an arepa; Sunday's stew uses carrot instead of camote and 25 g of rice; hominy is 105 to 110 g.
- Big adds are low-GL, protein-led snacks, so Big does not grow the meat (largest raw meat or fish in one serving is 270 g).
