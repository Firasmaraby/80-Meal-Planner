# North American Kitchen: draft week (names and one-line ideas)

Comfort food made bright. Every dish is something a Canadian or American home cook already makes, with a little more vegetable, bean or fish in it. This is the final week, after the lab.

Places: Canada (Québec-style split pea soup, Maritime salmon cakes, back bacon, baked beans), New England (sheet-pan chicken with apples), the South (blackened-chicken Cobb), the Midwest (wild rice and mushroom hotdish with bison), the Pacific Northwest (maple-mustard cedar-plank-style salmon), and classic diner and American kitchen food (Cobb salad, bison burger, turkey chili).

Carbs: oats, wild rice, whole-wheat bread and bun, potatoes, sweet potato, squash, beans, split peas. "Whole grains too".

Fish: Friday Maritime salmon cakes (canned salmon with bones) and Sunday maple-mustard salmon, two days apart with Saturday between. Both are cooked fresh.

## Breakfasts (3 recipes)
- Egg and veggie breakfast muffins with toast and an orange (a tin baked Sunday, two portions). Mon, Tue (leftover).
- Steel-cut oats with blueberries, walnuts and maple (one pot cooked Tuesday night, kept up to 5 days). Wed, Fri, Sat.
- Egg, back bacon and cheddar breakfast sandwich on whole-wheat toast. Thu, Sun.

## Dinners and the lunches they make
1. Mon: Sheet-pan chicken thighs with carrots, squash, apples and kale. Makes 2, so Tuesday lunch.
2. Tue: Turkey and bean chili with sweet potato and spinach. Makes 2, so Wednesday lunch.
3. Wed: Wild rice and mushroom hotdish with bison, cranberries and walnuts. Makes 2, so Thursday lunch.
4. Thu: Québec-style split pea soup with back bacon, kale, spinach and toast. Makes 2, so Friday lunch.
5. Fri: Maritime salmon cakes with sweet potato wedges and dill slaw. 1 serving.
6. Sat: Maple baked beans with wilted kale on toast. Makes 2, so Sunday lunch.
7. Sun (weekend project): Maple-mustard roasted salmon (cedar plank optional), squash, Brussels sprouts, quick-pickled onion. 1 serving.

## Fresh lunches
- Mon: Bison burger on a whole-wheat bun with cider slaw and pickled onion.
- Sat: Big chopped Cobb-style salad with blackened chicken (medium-spicy, seared thigh).
- Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sun: leftovers.

## Snacks (2 a day, Light drops the second)
Blueberry yogurt parfait, crispy roasted white beans, avocado toast, orange wedges, veggie sticks with yogurt ranch dip, cranberry and sunflower seed mix, Greek yogurt with apple and walnuts. Thursday and Sunday have one snack. Big adds one more a day (sweet potato rounds, seed mix, apple and cheddar, baked apple, smoothie).

## Checks against GUIDE section 11
- Not of the cuisine: no chia or flax. Walnuts are in the oats, the hotdish and the yogurt snack only (plus the Big baked apple). Sunflower seeds go in the parfait and the seed mix.
- Renamed dishes: hotdish and Québec-style say what they are, and the cedar plank is a tip, with an inspiredBy line.
- Same flavour base twice in a day: maple is kept out of the Sunday snack, so Sunday has it only in the salmon glaze and the leftover beans.
- One vegetable everywhere: kale in three recipes plus the slaw, spinach in four, cabbage in two, apple in four.
- Protein crutches: eggs only where the dish has them (muffins, sandwich, Cobb, salmon cakes). Back bacon only in the sandwich, soup and Cobb.
- Lists match steps: raw meat, fish and rice; canned beans and canned salmon.
