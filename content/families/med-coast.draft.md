# Mediterranean Coast: draft week (names and one-line ideas)

Written before any grams, then revised after the lab, after the chef critic and final review, and again when the omega-6:3 rule moved from daily to weekly. This is the final week.

Places: Greece (lemon chicken, fakes, strapatsada, village salad, meze plate, shrimp saganaki), Southern Italy (salmon with tomatoes, olives and anchovy; Sicilian fava, orange and fennel salad), Spain (sardines on beans, espinacas con garbanzos, tortilla española, saffron barley).

Carbs: potatoes, chickpeas, fava, white beans, lentils, barley, oats and fruit. No bread, pasta or rice, so the week is whole-food carbs.

Fish: Tuesday (sardines), Thursday (salmon), Sunday (shrimp). Never two days in a row, and no fish is ever a leftover.

## Breakfasts (3 recipes)
- Overnight oats with Greek yogurt, figs and almonds (with a spoonful of chia). Mon, Wed, Fri.
- Strapatsada, Greek tomato and feta scrambled eggs with spinach. Tue, Thu, about 10 minutes.
- Spanish potato and kale tortilla with a plain tomato and cucumber salad. Saturday, makes 2, so Sunday morning is a cold slice.

## Dinners and the lunches they make
1. Mon: Greek lemon chicken (thighs) with oregano potatoes, red peppers and roasted kale. 2 servings, makes Tuesday's lunch.
2. Tue: Sardines (and a little anchovy) on garlicky white beans with kale and almonds. 1 serving.
3. Wed: Fakes, Greek lentil soup with barley, spinach and feta. 2 servings, makes Thursday's lunch.
4. Thu: Salmon with cherry tomatoes, olives and anchovy on lemony barley and kale. 1 serving.
5. Fri: Espinacas con garbanzos with almonds ground into the base. 2 servings, makes Saturday's lunch.
6. Sat (weekend project): Saffron barley with chicken, peas and red pepper (inspired by paella), topped with toasted walnuts. 2 servings, makes Sunday's lunch.
7. Sun: Shrimp saganaki with feta and lemony potatoes. 1 serving, fast.

## Lunches
- Mon: Greek meze plate with quick hummus, tzatziki and a boiled egg.
- Wed: Village-style salad with chickpeas, feta and a garlicky spoon of yogurt.
- Fri: Sicilian-style fava bean, orange and fennel salad with egg.
- Tue, Thu, Sat, Sun: leftovers.

## Snacks (2 a day, Light drops the second)
Yogurt with honey, figs and toasted almonds; orange and toasted almonds; tzatziki dippers; fig and almond bites; garlic mushrooms with soft eggs; dried figs and almonds; quick lemony hummus with fennel and cucumber; garlic mushrooms with lemon; yogurt with honey, walnuts and figs (Saturday and Sunday). Big adds yogurt with orange and honey (Mon, Tue), orange and toasted walnuts (Wed, Fri), dried figs and almonds (Sat) and a sardine tapa (Thu, Sun).

## What changed and why
- The omega-6:3 gate is now judged over the week (at most 4:1, no day above 6:1). The week passes at every band edge (weekly ratio 3.1 at Light 1400 to 4.0 at Big 3200) because of the oily fish, walnuts and chia in the oats, and by keeping omega-6 low: modest olive oil (3 to 7 g a dish), little tahini, chicken breast in the saffron barley, and homemade hummus.
- Raw buying weights: chicken, potatoes, salmon, shrimp, barley and lentils use raw or dry entries. Canned beans, chickpeas, fava beans, lentils, sardines and anchovies use canned entries.
- Eggs are whole eggs (50 g each).
- Mushrooms are only in two Spanish tapa snacks (for niacin), which work as a small savoury snack or a starter, not in the fakes or espinacas.
- The hummus batch is a whole can of no-salt-added chickpeas with 1 tbsp tahini, made Sunday night. The meze plate and the Thursday snack use portions of it.
- Pantry is 45 groceries, so bay leaf, cinnamon, dill, capers and carrot were dropped.
