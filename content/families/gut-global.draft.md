# Gut Health Kitchen (gut-global): draft week

Written before the grams, then revised after the lab. Style `gut`: fibre at least 35 g (30 below 1,800 kcal), at least 30 different plants, a fermented food on 5 or more days. This week has one on all 7.

Places: Korea (kimchi jjigae, bibimbap), Germany (lentil salad, trout with kale and sauerkraut, barley soup, bircher), the Mediterranean (sardines on white beans), India (oat upma, dal with raita, chickpea chaat) and Japan (miso soup bowl, miso salmon, tofu rice bowl).

Fish: trout Tuesday, salmon Thursday, sardines Friday (the leftover sardine beans are Saturday's lunch). Fresh fish is always cooked fresh; only the canned sardines are a leftover.

## Breakfasts (3 recipes)
- Kefir bircher with grated apple, ground flax, raspberries, walnuts and hazelnuts: Mon, Wed, Sat (made the night before).
- Savoury oat upma with carrot and spinach, a spoon of yogurt: Tue, Fri.
- Miso soup bowl with barley, tofu, kale, boiled egg and walnuts for crunch: Thu, Sun.

## Dinners and the lunches they make
1. Mon: kimchi, chicken and tofu jjigae (2 servings, Tuesday lunch).
2. Tue: roasted trout with caraway potatoes, kale and warm sauerkraut with apple.
3. Wed: whole masoor dal with spinach and tomato, cucumber raita, brown rice (2 servings, Thursday lunch).
4. Thu: miso-ginger salmon with sesame spinach and quick pickles.
5. Fri: sardines on garlicky white beans with spinach, dill and rye croutons (2 servings, Saturday lunch).
6. Sat (weekend project): chicken bibimbap with four vegetables and kimchi (2 servings, Sunday lunch).
7. Sun: barley and chicken soup with root vegetables and kale.

## Other lunches
Mon German lentil salad with egg and sauerkraut; Wed seared tofu rice bowl with miso-ginger glaze; Fri chickpea chaat bowl.

## Check against GUIDE section 11
- Not of the cuisine: every ingredient belongs to its dish. Walnuts and flax are in the bircher the way Swiss and German muesli has them; walnuts in miso soup follow Japanese kurumi; hazelnuts are German.
- Renamed dishes: jjigae, upma, gigantes, bibimbap and Graupensuppe all say "inspired by" and name the change (chicken in the jjigae, oats in the upma, sardines on the beans, brown rice in the bibimbap, chicken for sausage in the soup).
- Same flavour base twice in a day: Wednesday has miso at lunch (tofu bowl) and nothing miso at breakfast (bircher); Thursday and Sunday have miso soup at breakfast, and on Thursday the miso salmon is a different dish, so the miso is the glaze there. Kimchi shows up Mon, Tue, Sat and Sun but never twice in one day except Sunday's leftover bibimbap.
- One vegetable everywhere: spinach is in many dishes but never twice in a recipe; kale, kimchi cabbage, shiitake and cucumber rotate.
- Eggs: at Mon lunch (lentil salad), the jjigae, the Wed tofu bowl, the Thu and Sun miso bowl, bibimbap and a Friday snack. All are normal in those dishes.
- Dairy: kefir or yogurt in 12 slots, never more than 3 in a day.
- Lists match steps: everything cooked is listed raw; the batch grains are "from your batch"; canned beans and sardines are the only ready foods.

## What changed after the lab and the critic
- Omega: walnuts and ground flax in the bircher, miso bowl and snacks; sardines Friday. The walnuts stay in the miso bowl for crunch because sesame pushed the week over 6:1.
- Safety: bircher jars at most 2 days ahead; every egg cooked until the yolk is set; the jjigae leftover gets a fresh egg; bibimbap cucumber and kimchi packed separately; grain batch amounts and thaw rule in the jjigae tip; opened sardine tin kept 2 days.
- Text: kimchi label (fish sauce or shrimp, live, refrigerated); sauerkraut now the plain "pickled" entry so no "canned" shows; calcium supplement line; Light and Big text made true.
- Taste: kale in the miso bowl; snacks turned into small dishes; Big third snack is a potato and bean salad or a chickpea salad, not a second dip.
- Calcium headroom: every tofu is now 'firm tofu, any kind' with no calcium claim, because most Canadian firm tofu is calcium-set. The Wednesday bowl uses less tofu (110 g counted) with a second egg, the miso bowl 80 g, and the jjigae has more chicken. Stress test on a scratch copy with every tofu swapped to calcium-set (683 mg/100 g): the lab passes and no day exceeds 2,500 mg. Counted peak is at most 1,874 mg (Big 3200).
