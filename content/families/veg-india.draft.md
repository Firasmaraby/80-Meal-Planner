# Indian Lacto-vegetarian: draft week

Regions: Gujarat (kadhi khichdi, sambharo, dhokla, chaas), Punjab (rajma, palak paneer, kala chana, matar paneer, chilla, missi roti), Rajasthan (dal baati, missi roti, til-alsi laddoo), Tamil Nadu (curd rice, idli and sambar, podi), Karnataka (bisibele bath, kosambari, agase podi).

Lacto-vegetarian: no meat, poultry, fish or eggs. Milk, dahi, chaas and paneer are in the week every day, and they are the B12 plan. Fatty fish does not apply.

## Breakfasts (3 recipes rotate)
- Moong and besan chilla with mint-peanut chutney, soaked almonds and masala chai (Mon, Wed, Fri).
- Kanda poha with peanuts and peas, thick dahi, soaked almonds and masala chai (Tue, Thu).
- Idli with sambar and flax-peanut podi, soaked almonds on the side (Sat, makes Sun too, weekend project).

## Snacks (two a day at Standard, one on Thu and Sat)
Mon: moong sprouts chaat, soya chunk tikka bites. Tue: haldi doodh with almonds, soya chunk tikka bites. Wed: moong dal kosambari, apple and pomegranate chaat. Thu: til-alsi laddoo. Fri: apple and pomegranate chaat, moong sprouts chaat. Sat: rice kheer. Sun: haldi doodh with almonds, masala chaas.

Big adds a third snack each day: masala chaas (Mon), khaman dhokla (Tue, Sat), til-alsi laddoo (Wed), cucumber and mint raita (Thu), rice kheer (Fri), moong dal kosambari (Sun).

Light holds 1600-1800 only. It drops the second snack on Mon, Tue, Wed (the second) and Fri (the first). 1400-1600 does not pass: the days stop trimming at about 1500 to 1650 kcal because vitamin E, choline and niacin run out, and Sunday's B12 needs both dairy snacks.

## Check against GUIDE 11 and 12
- Eggs: none. Meat, fish, fish sauce, gelatine: none. Mustard oil: not used. Canola does the cooking, with a little ghee (12 slots, 1 to 7 g).
- Dairy: milk, dahi or paneer in 17 of 33 slots, never more than 3 in a day. Paneer is in only 3 slots (palak paneer on Tue dinner and Wed lunch, matar paneer on Sun dinner). Milk, dahi and chaas carry the B12 (paneer carries almost none), and every day clears 80% of 2.4 mcg with room to spare.
- Protein: dal, rajma, kala chana, besan, soya chunks or dahi at every lunch and dinner, 20% or more of calories every day at every size.
- Saturated fat: highest day is about 9% (paneer days), so ghee is 1 to 7 g a dish and paneer is 42 to 46 g.
- Omega-3 (ALA only): ground flax in the chilla chutney (Mon, Wed, Fri), the idli podi and the laddoo; walnuts in the apple chaat and laddoo. Weekly omega-6:3 about 4:1 to 4.5:1.
- Choline: dals, besan, onion, spinach and peas carry it. It is the lowest nutrient on the Standard days (87 to 100%), so no day was patched with an egg.
- Vitamin E was the hardest nutrient. Soaked almonds with breakfast (a very ordinary home habit), almond paste in palak and matar paneer, haldi doodh, kheer and the laddoo carry it. Almonds are in 9 recipes: the reviewer may want to look at that.
- Crutch counts: cucumber in 3 recipes (kachumber, kosambari and the Big-only raita), carrot in 4, spinach in 4 (and the chilla), tomato in 8, peanuts in 5 recipes. Nuts or peanuts are in 22 of 33 slots, but most are a spoonful (soaked almonds at breakfast, a peanut crunch on poha and chaat). No flax or walnuts on a savoury dish that did not already have them in the cuisine (flax is in an idli podi and a chilla chutney, both real).
- Groceries: 44 (limit 45), same at Big. Missing staples: none that matter. Sprouted moong is the library's "mung beans, sprouted"; thick dahi is "Greek yogurt, plain, low-fat"; idli rice is parboiled white rice; makhana would be lotus seeds (not used).
- Water in the steps is never a measured amount, so nothing needs to scale.
- No em or en dashes anywhere.
