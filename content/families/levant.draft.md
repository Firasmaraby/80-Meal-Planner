# Levantine Table: draft week (names and one-line ideas)

Written before any grams, then revised after the lab and again after the chef critic's report. This is the final week.

Places: Lebanon (shish tawook, tabbouleh, fattoush, adas bi hamod, samke harra, kibbeh, balila), Syria (ijjeh, kibbeh), Palestine (musakhan, mujadara, tomato and chili sardines, kafta), Jordan (foul with tahini, the za'atar and olive oil breakfast, halloumi).

Carbs: lentils, chickpeas, fava, bulgur and whole-wheat pita. No white rice and no potatoes, so the week is "whole grains too" (bulgur and whole-wheat pita are the whole grains).

Fish: Wednesday (salmon samke harra), Friday (canned sardines in spicy tomato) and Sunday (mackerel with tarator). Never back to back, and no fish is ever a leftover.

Staples used for real, from the curated list: za'atar, sumac, baharat, pomegranate molasses and labneh. Still missing from the library: freekeh, Aleppo pepper and pickled turnip.

## Breakfasts (4 recipes)
- Foul medames with two boiled eggs, tahini, raw onion and radish. Mon, Thu. One 400 g tin makes both mornings.
- Za'atar pita with labneh, olives, egg and cucumber (man'oushe style). Tue, Fri.
- Balila, warm chickpeas with tahini, lemon, cumin and a boiled egg. Wed.
- Ijjeh, a herb and chard omelette with a little halloumi. Sat, makes 2, so Sunday morning is a cold wedge in pita.

## Dinners and the lunches they make
1. Mon: Shish tawook with parsley tabbouleh and toum-style garlic sauce. 2 servings, makes Tuesday's lunch.
2. Tue: Mujadara hamra with caramelised onions and malfouf salad. 2 servings, makes Wednesday's lunch.
3. Wed: Samke harra, spicy cilantro and walnut baked salmon with tomato bulgur (burghul bi banadoura). 1 serving.
4. Thu: Adas bi hamod, lemony lentil and chard soup. 2 servings, makes Friday's lunch.
5. Fri: Spicy tomato sardines on toasted pita with parsley and onion salad. 1 serving.
6. Sat (weekend project): Baked kibbeh with spiced lamb and walnut filling and tomato sumac salad. 2 servings, makes Sunday's lunch.
7. Sun: Baked mackerel on collapsed eggplant with tahini tarator. 1 serving.

## Lunches

- Thu: Lamb kafta pita with roasted cauliflower, tarator and tomato.
- Sat: Musakhan-style sumac chicken and onions on pita.
- Tue, Wed, Fri, Sun: leftovers.

## Snacks (2 a day)
Figs stuffed with walnuts (Mon, Thu); pomegranate bowl with mint (Mon); pomegranate with walnuts (Tue, Sat); dried figs and almonds (Tue, Sun); grilled halloumi, cucumber and mint in pita (Wed, Thu); yogurt with figs and almonds (Wed, Sat); hummus with radish and cucumber, a real batch recipe made Thursday evening (Fri, Sun); pomegranate with toasted almonds (Fri). Big adds za'atar pita with olives (Mon, Thu), pita with olives, cucumber and mint (Tue, Sun), fig and tahini pita (Wed, Fri) and a labneh, olive and mint pita roll (Sat).

## Check against GUIDE section 11
- Not of the cuisine: no chia, flax, canola or tuna. Walnuts only where Levantine cooks use them: samke harra, kibbeh filling, optional in ijjeh, and with figs or pomegranate on the snack tray.
- A dish renamed into something it isn't: mujadara hamra is already bulgur, musakhan says "style" (boneless thighs, less oil), and the labneh plate says thick yogurt can stand in.
- Protein crutches: yogurt and labneh are a sauce or a side, in 6 slots a week and never more than 2 in a day. Eggs are at every breakfast, which is the one thing I could not fix: men need 440 mg of choline a day and an olive-oil week without organ meat gets there with an egg each morning.
- Same flavour base twice in a day: the cilantro soup (Thu) is never on the day of the cilantro fish (Wed). The one overlap left is Monday, where the sumac and pomegranate molasses fattoush at lunch meets the parsley tabbouleh at dinner. Every other order I tried broke calcium, vitamin E or the omega gate on another day.
- One vegetable everywhere: cauliflower, eggplant, cabbage, carrot, chard, radish and romaine join tomato and cucumber, and the salads change every day.
- Weird breakfasts: foul, za'atar pita with labneh, balila and ijjeh are all everyday Levantine breakfasts.
- Lists match steps: raw weights for chicken, lamb, fish, lentils, bulgur and eggplant. Canned only for fava, chickpeas and sardines. Eggs are whole eggs.
- Untrue claims: only what the dish carries. The curated staples (za'atar, sumac, baharat, pomegranate molasses, labneh) are used for flavour, not as nutrient sources.
- Silent sub-recipes: the hummus batch, the toum-style sauce, the tarator and the za'atar paste have their steps inside the dish that uses them.

## What changed and why (after the critic)
- Yogurt is no longer the default sauce. Kafta and mackerel get tarator, tawook gets a toum-style garlic sauce, mujadara is plain with its cabbage salad.
- Real staples: za'atar, sumac, baharat, pomegranate molasses and labneh now carry the flavour. Foul is two mornings, with balila and a man'oushe-style plate as the other breakfasts.
- Olive oil is back as the cooking fat. The omega-6:3 rule (6:1 over the week, 9:1 any day) is met with three fatty-fish dinners and the walnut dishes, and the weekly ratio is about 5 to 5.8 at every band edge.
- A real Palestinian dish (musakhan-style sumac chicken) and a Jordanian touch (foul with tahini, the za'atar and olive oil breakfast).
- The fish dishes are salmon samke harra, canned sardines in tomato and mackerel with tarator and collapsed eggplant. Tuna fattoush became a chickpea and halloumi fattoush with real sumac.
- Calcium is rebuilt with tahini, halloumi, canned sardines with bones, labneh, sesame and herbs, with no extra yogurt.
- Light is dropped. With the corrected yogurt calcium, Tuesday, Wednesday and Saturday stay at 65 to 75% calcium at 1600 and 1800 kcal, and Monday and Sunday cannot be trimmed below about 1810 kcal.
- Pantry is exactly 45 groceries, so potato, orange, apple, dates, apricots, pistachios and pickles were dropped.
