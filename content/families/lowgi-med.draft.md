# Diabetes-friendly Mediterranean: draft week (names and one-line ideas)

Written after the family file and revised after every lab run. This is the final week.

Places: Greece (fasolada, souvlaki with horiatiki, fakes salata, yogurt with walnuts, psari plaki), Crete (dakos-style lunch), southern Italy (orzotto, pasta e ceci, frittata, sarde alla griglia), Spain (tostada con tomate, lentejas, tosta de sardinas, mejillones, garbanzos, caballa with pisto).

Carbs: one small, honest starch at each of the three meals, always beside beans, lentils, eggs, fish or chicken. Rye is a thin slice, barley is a few spoonfuls, farro a spoonful, whole-wheat pasta a small handful in a soup that is mostly chickpeas, pita a few bites. No juice, no honey, no sugar. Carb style: whole grains too.

Fish: Tuesday (fresh sardines), Friday (salmon) and Sunday (mackerel) never touch, and are always cooked fresh. Canned sardines on rye (Thursday and Saturday snacks) and canned mussels on rye (Monday and Wednesday snacks) cover B12 and calcium on the other days.

## Breakfasts (3 recipes)
- Tostada con tomate with two soft eggs and an orange. Mon, Tue, Thu. Tomato grated the night before.
- Greek yogurt with strawberries, walnuts and toasted oats. Wed, Fri.
- Spinach and herb frittata with tomato and rye. Sat (slower, with red pepper, mushrooms and spinach), Sun is the cold leftover wedge.

## Dinners and the lunches they make
1. Mon: Barley risotto (orzotto) with chicken, mushrooms, broccoli and cannellini, arugula salad (chicken and mushrooms browned in a second pan while the barley simmers). 2 servings, Tuesday's lunch.
2. Tue: Grilled sardines on a white bean, tomato and arugula salad. 1 serving, fresh.
3. Wed: Chicken souvlaki with horiatiki and tzatziki, a few bites of pita. 2 servings, Thursday's lunch.
4. Thu: Spanish lentil stew (lentejas) with a little cured chorizo, carrot and spinach, finished with sherry vinegar. 2 servings, Friday's lunch.
5. Fri: Baked salmon with tomatoes, broccoli, zucchini and Kalamata olives over a little farro (a Greek-style tray bake, in the spirit of psari plaki). 1 serving, fresh.
6. Sat (weekend project): Fasolada, the Greek white bean soup, from dry beans soaked overnight, with olives and an arugula salad. 2 servings, Sunday's lunch.
7. Sun: Seared mackerel on pisto with chickpeas and a thin slice of rye. 1 serving, fresh.

## Lunches cooked fresh
- Mon: Fakes salata, the Greek lentil salad with feta.
- Wed: Pasta e ceci with more ceci than pasta (broccoli cooked in the pot).
- Sat: Cretan dakos-style salad with chickpeas, tomato, feta and a few walnuts.
- Tue, Thu, Fri, Sun: leftovers.

## Snacks (2 a day, Light keeps the first, Big adds a third)
Mon mussels on rye with onion and sherry vinegar, lupini; Tue roasted chickpeas, strawberries with almonds; Wed mussels on rye, orange with walnuts; Thu sardines on rye, cannellini dip with veg sticks; Fri Greek yogurt with hazelnuts, roasted chickpeas; Sat cannellini dip, Greek yogurt with hazelnuts; Sun lupini, strawberries with almonds. Big adds olives with almonds (Mon, Tue), cannellini crostini (Wed, Sat), orange with walnuts (Thu) and strawberries with walnuts (Fri, Sun).

## Light size
Light claims 1400-1600 and 1600-1800. I tried 1200-1400 and the lab refused it: Friday, Saturday and Sunday would not size below about 1,320 to 1,430 kcal, and Thursday dropped under the 30 g fibre line at 1,200, so the week cannot honestly claim it without trimming the beans and vegetables that make it work. At 1,400 the pita is 12 g (a few bites), the rye 16 to 25 g (a thin slice or half), the barley 25 g and the farro 15 g dry, which the notes call "a few bites" and "a thin slice". Nothing is shrunk to a crumb.

## Check against GUIDE sections 11 and 12
- Cuisine fit: every dish is something a home cook in Greece, Italy or Spain would put on the table. Names are honest: the dakos is "dakos-style" (rye toast in place of the barley rusk, chickpeas added), the orzotto is orzotto with chicken, the plaki is "plaki style" with a spoon of farro, and the pisto is pisto with mackerel on top.
- Carbs are spread: each main meal has one small starch with protein and fibre beside it. Fruit always comes with nuts or yogurt.
- Legumes at two meals on most days: lentils, chickpeas, cannellini, white beans and lupini.
- Acid and vegetables first: an arugula, tomato or cucumber salad sits beside most dinners, with lemon or red wine vinegar.
- No juice, no honey, no dried fruit, no added sugar.
- Weeknight honesty: the orzotto (Mon) and lentejas (Thu) are the longest weeknight pots, both mostly hands-off. Fasolada (Sat) is the only project dish and has a pressure-cooker tip.
- Flavour bases: no day repeats the same base twice. Tomato appears in many dishes (it is the cuisine), but only one meal a day leans on tomato and chickpea together.
- Crutch counts (Standard): eggs 5 slots (three tostadas and the Saturday frittata with its Sunday leftover); dairy 11 slots (yogurt breakfasts and snacks, feta in the lentil salad, Parmesan in the orzotto and the pasta e ceci, tzatziki and feta with the souvlaki, feta on the dakos); nuts 9 slots (walnuts where Greek cooking uses them, hazelnuts as a Piedmont and Sicilian touch, almonds in a snack); fatty fish 5 slots (three fresh dinners and two canned sardine snacks), plus two mussel snacks.
- Fat is olive oil throughout. No flax, chia, canola or butter.

## What changed after the lab and the read-through
- Light 1200-1400 does not pass, so Light claims 1400-1800.
- Choline and B12 on yogurt days: the first draft put yogurt at four breakfasts, and the lab kept flagging choline and B12 on those days. Eggs moved to Monday, Tuesday and Thursday, and canned mussels and sardines took the Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Saturday snacks. The orzotto got chicken and a lot of broccoli (both choline sources), and the pasta e ceci got broccoli too.
- Grocery cap: celery was dropped to stay under the grocery cap, dill became dried dill (a pantry herb), pistachios gave way to hazelnuts for vitamin E, and the pear snack went because Sunday needed more protein.
- Oil stays at 12 g or less per serving in the big pots so Big never goes over the 27 g cap.
- Saturday's frittata is three eggs per portion so Saturday and Sunday reach 20% protein.
