## GUIDE section 11 and 12 check

- Not of the cuisine: no flax, chia or walnuts. Hazelnuts and sunflower seeds are Nordic, sesame is Japanese. Rapeseed (canola) oil is the
  everyday fat, with a little butter. UV mushrooms stand in for plain mushrooms for vitamin D.
- Renamed dishes carry "inspired" notes: ärtsoppa without pork (smoked paprika for the smoke), lohikeitto with milk, tamago-toji with tofu,
  nabe with less miso and no sake, jaga-bata with tofu. Pearl barley stands in for rice (mugi-gohan) and keeps the glycaemic load down.
- Protein crutches: eggs in 7 slots from 5 dishes (herring plate, egg smørrebrød, tofu and egg bowl, potato salad, scrambled eggs), the
  men's choline target needs them on almost every day. Skyr is a sauce or base, not piled on top.
- Flavour bases: miso in 6 dishes but never twice in one meal; Thursday has the tofu and egg bowl (soy, ginger) then the miso mackerel at dinner.
- One vegetable everywhere: potato in 5 dishes, cucumber in 5, spinach in 4, carrot in 4. Fruit is spread (apple, blueberry, pear).
- Weird breakfasts: porridge, overnight muesli, egg smørrebrød and a Japanese miso set are all real breakfasts. The miso set is the Saturday one.
- Batch staples: pea soup (45 minutes), barley (cooled within 1 hour), eggs boiled for the week all say how long they keep.
- Revision after the critic: listeria text now covers pregnant, over 65 and weakened immune system (heat to 74 C); opened packs
  within 3 days or by date, small packs; Atlantic not Baltic; mackerel never king, thaw line; frozen-berry caution; bonito dashi (no kombu)
  in the mackerel, hot pot and miso breakfast; breakfast pickle is daikon and the potato salad uses carrot (less cucumber, 45 groceries).
- Selenium: peak day is day 4 (tofu and egg bowl lunch plus mackerel dinner) at 351 ug at Big 3200 (88% of the 400 ug UL), 316 at Big 2800,
  281 at Big 2400. Trimming the mackerel and barley moved it by under 15 ug, so it is kept.
- Final review: Sunday's at-risk option is now fresh pan-fried salmon (Tuesday's would be 5 days old); herring warmed in a covered pan; mackerel
  wash line after handling; howItWorks dashi and Light/Big text corrected; mug soup 9 minutes; muesli tip "mix it the night before".
- Tofu stress test (all firm tofu swapped for calcium-set, 683 mg/100 g): passes at every edge. To get there tofu is 60 to 90 g a serving,
  tofu notes say "any kind", no text claims calcium from tofu, the mug soup has no tofu, and Sunday's tofu carries fried eggs for protein.
  Calcium peak, counted / stressed: Big 3200 1936 / 2483 mg (limit 2500), Big 2800 1824 / 2354, Big 2400 1743 / 2286.
- Selenium at Big 3200 peaks at 349 ug (day 4), unchanged, kept.
- Slot counts (Standard): dairy-forward about 9 (skyr and milk bases), eggs 7, shellfish none.
