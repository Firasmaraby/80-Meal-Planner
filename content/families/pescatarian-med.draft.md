# pescatarian-med draft

One fish or seafood main meal a day; the other main meal is legumes, eggs or vegetables. Sardines on toast appear as a snack on Thu and Fri. Fish portions grow at Big.

| Day | Breakfast | Lunch | Dinner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Baked eggs in tomato and pepper | Shrimp saganaki | Pasta e fagioli (makes 2) |
| Tue | Fig and almond oats | Pasta e fagioli (leftover) | Grilled sardines, lemon potatoes, greens |
| Wed | Baked eggs in tomato and pepper | Portuguese chickpea and tuna salad | Smoky lentils with chard (makes 2) |
| Thu | Fig and almond oats | Smoky lentils (leftover) | Baked cod, chickpeas, spinach (makes 2) |
| Fri | Baked eggs in tomato and pepper | Baked cod (leftover) | Briam with white beans and feta (makes 2) |
| Sat | Ricotta, apple and walnut toast | Briam (leftover) | Portuguese fish stew, weekend project (makes 2) |
| Sun | Fig and almond oats | Fish stew (leftover) | Zucchini, potato and ricotta frittata |

Checks against GUIDE section 11 and 12: no recipe more than twice except breakfasts; 44 groceries; fatty fish twice (Tue sardines, Sat salmon stew) plus canned sardines Thu and Fri snacks; no high-mercury fish, no liver; iodized salt as the salt; no-salt-added beans; shellfish and fish doneness written in the steps.

Light claims 1600-1800 only: 1400-1600 was dropped because Monday (pasta e fagioli), Wednesday (lentils) and Saturday (briam) cannot be trimmed to 1400 kcal without cutting the beans and fish that carry iron, folate, niacin and B12.

Big snacks (each recipe at most twice): Mon tzatziki, Tue and Wed tomato-bean toast, Thu dates and almonds, Fri boiled egg with tomato, Sat tzatziki, Sun chickpea-tomato cup. The tuna toast was dropped to keep selenium off the seafood-heavy days 1 and 7 (peak 329 ug at Big 3200).
