## Checks against GUIDE 11 and 12
- Fatty fish: sardine snack day 1, mackerel day 3, salmon day 5, sardine snack day 7. All on non-adjacent days.
- Lamb: one lamb stew (day 7) and kofta that is mostly lean beef with a smaller share of lean lamb (day 2, leftover day 3). Two dishes.
- Eggs: breakfast on 4 of 7 days (shakshuka x2, ful x2), yogurt bowls on the other 3. Egg snack only on day 2 (a yogurt day) and day 7.
- Dairy: kept to about 11 slots. No yogurt in the tawook marinade and no feta in shakshuka, to keep it down.
- Tomato base: breakfast and kofta sauce share a day only lightly (ful is garnished with fresh tomato); the mackerel pilaf is herb and lemon, not tomato.
- Omega: walnuts only in the yogurt bowl and a yogurt snack (a real Levantine and Gulf pairing); fish days carry the rest. Tahini and nuts kept small.
- Zinc, phosphorus, selenium, iron: watched at XL. Egg white eggah and a leaner chicken and lamb split keep phosphorus under 4000 mg at 3700.
- Bands: Standard 2200-2400 and 2400-2800, Big 2800-3200, XL 3200-3700. XL 3700-4200 not claimed: phosphorus, selenium, zinc and iron go over their upper limits and the kofta goes over 400 g of raw meat.
