# keto-middle-east draft: Keto Middle Eastern

Lebanon, Syria, Turkey and the Gulf, without bread, rice, bulgur or sugar. Fat comes from olive oil, avocado, olives, tahini, nuts where the dish has them, and fish, not from cheese or cream. Protein is lean on purpose (chicken breast, lean beef, sea bass, anchovies, sardines, trout, a little lean lamb), because olive oil, tahini and eggs are already about 14% to 34% saturated. Shrimp is out (the library's raw shrimp is very high in sodium) and so is pork.

## Chef critic revision: what changed

- **Fixes applied:** 1 (fattoush has no hazelnuts, more oil, radish, cucumber and avocado cubes), 2 (cilbir: no walnuts, more yogurt and olive oil, olives up), 4 (shakshuka: no hazelnuts, saucier, more oil), 5 (breakfast order: Thu labneh, Fri cilbir, Sat shakshuka), 6 (labneh plate: no walnuts, more labneh, oil, olives and mint), 8 (anchovies: hazelnut garnish replaced by a walnut and hazelnut tarator, more parsley; the tip leads with fresh sardines), 10 (za'atar olives: no walnuts, more olives and oil), 11 (no collard greens anywhere; Circassian chicken and shawarma lose their raw greens and get more romaine; fattoush and anchovy salad use kale), 12 to 16 (more oil in Circassian paprika oil, kofta with some oil kneaded in, sis marinated an hour, shawarma reheated in a hot pan, samke harra sauce), 17 (the Big-only smashed avocado, olives and walnuts snack is gone) and 18 (howItWorks).
- **Deviations, because the lab would not pass:**
  - Fix 3 (karniyarik): the walnuts stay (a small portion); oil, tomato and pepper are only slightly up. Dropping them put Wednesday and Thursday over 40 g net carbs at Big, and pine nuts (the traditional garnish) broke the omega ratio.
  - Fix 7 (sea bass): hazelnuts stay in the rice. Swapping them for almonds and adding dakkous tomato pushed Thursday's net carbs over 40 g at Big.
  - Fix 9 (artichokes): hazelnuts stay, as the Turkish nut snack. Without them Friday's saturated fat is 10.3% to 11%.
  - Fix 17 (Big portions): the Big 2800-3200 band is dropped (final review fix 1): at 3200 the largest raw portion reached 395 g and saturated fat sat at 9.96%. At 2800 the largest raw portion is 350 g, not 300 g; writing the mains smaller pushed saturated fat over 10%.
  - Added to cover what the nut cuts took out: chia in the labneh plate (stirred in to thicken, as a chia-labneh bowl), walnut and hazelnut tarator on the anchovies, walnut tarator on the kofta, and muhammara as a Wednesday and Saturday snack.
  - Cilbir has one egg, not two: two eggs put Wednesday and Friday over the saturated fat limit.
- **Light dryness:** every lean main still has a sauce or dressing at Light (tahini, walnut tarator, dakkous, garlic yogurt, lemon-olive oil).

## GUIDE section 11 check

- **Eggs:** an egg at all 7 breakfasts (shakshuka x2 with 2 eggs, cilbir x2 and labneh plate x3 with 1 egg). No egg snacks. The gate forces it: without the egg the three labneh-plate days fall below 70% of the male choline target.
- **Dairy slots:** 5 a week (labneh plate x3, cilbir x2), never more than 1 a day.
- **Nuts and seeds:** 26 of 35 Standard slots hold a nut, seed or tahini (walnuts 13, hazelnuts 8, tahini 6, chia 3, pumpkin seeds 1), down from 33. Walnuts sit in the Circassian chicken, olive dip, muhammara, tarators, samke harra and the spiced nut snack, with a small amount in the karniyarik. Hazelnuts are the Turkish snack, the sea bass rice and the artichoke meze.
- **Chia:** only the 3 labneh plates; flax none.
- **One vegetable everywhere:** kale in 6 recipes (shakshuka, fattoush, sis, karniyarik, anchovy salad, trout), spinach 1, avocado in 5 recipes and never 3 dishes in one day.
- **Raw weights:** meat and fish are raw. Sardines are canned and drained, olives are jarred and rinsed.
- **Meat cap:** the largest raw meat or fish portion at Big 2800 is 350 g.
- **Final review fixes applied:** Big band 2800-3200 dropped, Thursday and Sunday Big snacks, more tomato in the karniyarik and the dakkous, honest wording for the eggs at Big, fattoush and kofta names, avocado, chia, hamsi tarator, muhammara, trout and inspiredBy notes, and howItWorks (fatty fish three times, more places for nuts).

## GUIDE section 12 check (Keto)

- No grains, bread, rice, potato, sugar or beans. Pomegranate molasses is out.
- Net carbs: 28 to 35 g a day at Standard and Light, up to 39.4 g at Big 2800 (limit 40).
- Saturated fat: the worst day at each band edge is 9.6% to 9.96%. Oil is 4 to 12 g a dish.
- Omega-6:3: weekly 3.8 to 4.7 across sizes, worst day 8.0:1 (Light 1400).
- Sodium: at most about 2,370 mg a day at Standard and about 2,880 mg at Big 2800. Olives never top 75 g a day.
