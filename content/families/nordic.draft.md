## GUIDE section 11 check

- Not of the cuisine: no flax, chia or walnuts. Hazelnuts are Danish and Swedish and sit in the sweet places (porridge,
  skyr bowl, the apple snack). Sunflower seeds go on the salads and slaw. Rapeseed (canola) oil is the everyday fat.
  The yellow peas are the whole yellow pea Swedes use for ärtsoppa. The vitamin D mushrooms are a tweak on plain svampmacka
  and pytt, and ordinary mushrooms work.
- Renamed dishes: lapskaus and hønsekødssuppe carry "inspired" notes (barley and kale added, barley instead of
  dumplings). Skyr stands in for the mayonnaise on the smørrebrød and makes a light stand-in for remoulade. Kefir stands in for
  filmjölk. Lingonberry jam, Swedish sweet mustard and rye flakes are the curated staples. The gravlax is a lighter cure, because the lab caps salt at 2.5 g.
- Protein crutches: eggs only where the dish has them (herring plate, smørrebrød x2, pea soup pancakes x2, the fishcake
  binder, pytt), 7 slots in 5 dishes, about one a day. Shrimp is in 4 slots (two breakfasts, two snacks); mushrooms in 3 (days 1, 5, 6).
- Same flavour base twice in a day: Thursday has allspice and dill salmon soup at lunch and marjoram pea soup at dinner.
  Friday's lunch is that pea soup and dinner is dill and nutmeg fishcakes. Lingonberry is the one repeat, as a bright
  accent at breakfast and dinner on Monday and Thursday, and dill shows up at breakfast and dinner on Sunday.
- One vegetable everywhere: potato in 5 dishes (meatballs, lapskaus, lohikeitto, pytt, gravlax), carrot in 4, kale in
  3, cucumber in 4, radish in 2, apple in 4, beets in 4. Parsley and dill are herbs and are used by the handful.
- Weird breakfasts: porridge, skyr with berries and open-faced rye are all Nordic breakfasts.
- Batch staples: the gravlax says to start on Saturday morning, with the cure time and how long it keeps. The peas say to soak
  overnight. The meatballs, soups and stews each say to pack the second portion.
- Slot counts (Standard): egg 7, dairy-forward 9 (skyr bowls, porridge with skyr, kefir, cheese) with skyr or milk as a
  sauce or base in about 7 more, fatty fish 4 slots from 3 cooks, shrimp 4.
- Sizes: Light covers 1600-1800 only. At 1400 the days stop trimming at about 1480 to 1650 kcal, because vitamin E, iron
  and calcium sit on the 80% line after the fats, grains and dairy are cut.
