## Check against GUIDE section 11 and 12

- Fatty fish: sardines Fri, salmon Sun. Non-adjacent. Tuna and cod are lean fish on top.
- Protein at 30%: every meal built on a lean protein (chicken breast, cod, turkey, shrimp, pork tenderloin, tuna, nonfat Greek yogurt, egg whites, lupini). Lentils and chickpeas carry fibre and iron.
- Fibre 30 g: legumes at most meals, big plates of salad, fennel, zucchini, cabbage, spinach.
- Volume first: salads Mon and Fri, soups Sat, stew Tue, roasted vegetables Sun.
- Crutches: eggs at 4 breakfast slots only, no egg in dinners; yogurt slots about 8; feta kept to 30 g a serving; nuts only walnuts in a Greek yogurt bowl and an orange snack; fruit varied (pear, orange, tomato).
- Flavour bases: Greek (oregano, lemon, mint), Italian (fennel, tomato, capers), Spanish (smoked paprika, sherry vinegar). No base repeated within a day.
- Weekday breakfasts 10 min or less; weekend frittata is the slower breakfast. Dinners 45 min or less hands-on; souvlaki is the weekend project.
- Light: drop the second snack on days 1 to 5; pita stays 15 g or more at Light, and the oats and almonds were cut from the yogurt bowl because they shrank to crumbs.
- Staples used: sumac, Aleppo, Kalamata olives, sherry vinegar. Za'atar and labneh skipped on purpose (dairy slots, and nothing in the week wanted za'atar).

- Critic round: tuna tomato snack removed (tomato and seafood pile-up on days 3 and 5), tuna salad uses fennel not tomato, keftedes get grated zucchini, soup gets oregano and bay, frittata bakes at 180 C, salmon 150 g.
- Final review round: wild sockeye for the salmon tray, oregano on the dip, dried figs snack on day 4, honest Light paragraph in howItWorks.
