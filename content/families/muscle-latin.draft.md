## Checks against GUIDE 11 and 12
- Fatty fish: trout ceviche day 3, sardine tostadas day 5, salmon moqueca day 7. All non-adjacent.
- Eggs: whole eggs at 4 of 7 breakfasts (rancheros x2, pericos x2), plus the boiled-egg snack once at Standard and twice at Big and XL. Egg whites are in 6 slots at Standard and 8 at Big and XL. They are kept as the low-phosphorus protein; XL day 7 has two egg-white snacks, and swapping one for sardines failed phosphorus (4,045 mg).
- Dairy: about 9 breakfast and snack slots; at most one cottage cheese a day.
- Phosphorus is the XL limit: 3,812 mg at 3700 kcal (limit 4,000). Selenium, zinc and iron have 14 to 20% margin.
- Tightest margins at XL 3700: day 2 protein 30.0%, day 3 glycaemic load 181.
- Salt trimmed for sodium: feijoada 0.7 g, rancheros 0.9 g, pericos 0.8 g.
- Bands claimed: Standard 2200-2400 and 2400-2800, Big 2800-3200, XL 3200-3700. XL 3700-4200 not claimed (phosphorus, selenium, zinc over their limits).
- Missing staples: choclo (Peruvian corn), manioc flour (farofa, pirão) and dendê. Frozen corn stands in for choclo, and the toasted corn is only a nutty garnish.
