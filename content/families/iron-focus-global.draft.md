# Iron Focus: draft week

Places: Canada (chili over a baked potato, bran flakes with ground flax and a boiled egg), India (tofu bhurji, palak masoor dal), the Mediterranean (sardine pasta, mussels, lamb and chickpea stew, smashed sardines on rye, sabich-style egg and chickpea pita), Latin America (huevos a la mexicana, mackerel with salsa verde on lentils), China (tofu and broccoli stir-fry).

Style: iron-focus, 18 mg from food every day. Standard 2000 kcal covers 1800-2400, Light 1600 covers 1600-1800. No Big.

Iron: red meat twice (Monday chili, Saturday lamb stew, each eaten twice), sardines, mussels, lentils, chickpeas and beans, tofu, pumpkin seeds, spinach and kale, a modest fortified bran flakes breakfast. No liver. Every iron meal and snack carries vitamin C (peppers, tomato, orange, strawberries, broccoli, lemon, lime, kale). No tea or coffee anywhere. Dairy sits mostly at snacks, with a few spoonfuls of yogurt on the bran bowl. No yogurt in the dal.

Fish: sardines Tuesday dinner and Friday lunch, Atlantic mackerel Sunday dinner, mussels Thursday dinner. Fish is never a leftover.
