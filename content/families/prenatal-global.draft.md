# Pregnancy Global Kitchen: draft week (names and one-line ideas)

Warm home cooking for pregnancy, mostly Canadian and American with dishes from Mexico, Italy and Japan. Everything is cooked through, pasteurized or heated, and written under the sodium ceiling with iodized salt. This is the final week, after the lab.

Places: Canada (oatmeal, traybake chicken, maple-mustard salmon, egg salad, diner hash), the US (beef and bean chili), Mexico (tomato eggs with beans, enchilada bake), Italy (red lentil soup, lemon salmon pasta), Japan (ginger-soy trout with edamame and spinach), plus a Greek-style chickpea bowl.

Carbs: fortified instant oats, whole-wheat bread and pasta, brown rice, corn tortillas, potatoes, beans. "Whole grains too".

Fish: Tue salmon, Fri trout, Sun salmon (never back to back), plus canned light tuna on Monday. No albacore, no smoked fish.

## Breakfasts (plain, easy, queasy-morning friendly)
- Oatmeal with blueberries and walnuts (a little iron-fortified instant oats with rolled oats): Mon, Sun
- Yogurt bowl with fruit and toasted oats: Thu, Fri
- Firm scrambled eggs and spinach on toast with orange and milk: Tue
- Mexican-style tomato eggs with beans and corn tortillas: Wed
- Potato, pepper and spinach hash with firm eggs (weekend): Sat

## Dinners and the lunches they make
1. Mon: lemon-herb chicken traybake (2 portions, Tue lunch)
2. Tue: maple-mustard salmon, brown rice, broccoli
3. Wed: Tuscan-style red lentil and spinach soup (2 portions, Thu lunch)
4. Thu: beef and black bean chili (2 portions, Fri lunch)
5. Fri: ginger-soy trout, rice, edamame, soy-dressed spinach
6. Sat (weekend project): chicken and black bean enchilada bake (2 portions, Sun lunch)
7. Sun: lemon salmon pasta with broccoli and spinach

## Fresh lunches
Mon open-faced light tuna and chickpea toast with a spinach salad, Wed Greek-style chickpea and cucumber bowl, Sat egg salad sandwich with cucumber. Other lunches are leftovers.

## Snacks (2 a day)
Orange and sunflower seeds, edamame, peanut butter oat bites, veggie sticks and yogurt dip, maple walnut and seed mix, blueberry-ginger yogurt, blueberry-banana yogurt smoothie, egg and tomato toast, crispy cumin chickpeas, avocado toast, banana with peanut butter.

## Decisions
- Standard only: Big is dropped because iron from food must stay between 21.6 and 29 mg at every edge; bigger portions pass 29 mg at 2800.
- Iron: kept between 21.6 and 29 mg a day (the prenatal vitamin adds more). A little iron-fortified instant oats sits in the oatmeal and the oat bites; everything else uses rolled oats.
- Groceries trimmed to the 45 cap: no feta, hummus, mozzarella, kale, celery, pears, strawberries or almonds.
- Caffeine: none in any recipe. No alcohol, no herbal teas, no seaweed, no liver, no deli meat, no soft-ripened cheese.
- Food safety: fish cooked to 158 F (70 C), ground beef 160 F (71 C), poultry and reheated leftovers 165 F (74 C), leftovers eaten within 2 days; produce rinsed before cutting; hands, board and knife washed after raw meat, fish and eggs.
