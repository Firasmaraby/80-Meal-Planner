# healthy-aging-global draft (revised after critic and safety review)

Style `healthy-aging`, graded for a woman and a man of 65. At least 25 g of protein at every breakfast, lunch and dinner. Cuisines: Canadian, Mediterranean, Japanese, British. Soft textures, cooking for one or two, single portions that freeze (not the chowder).

| Day | Breakfast | Lunch | Dinner | Snacks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Soft eggs, tomato white beans, toast, milk | Red lentil soup (cooks 2) | Baked salmon, dill, mash, spinach | Pear, almond butter, honey; apple oatmeal |
| 2 | Okayu with canned salmon and egg | Lentil soup (leftover) | Shepherd's pie, lamb and lentils (cooks 2) | Yogurt, ginger apple, nuts; pear, almond butter |
| 3 | Oat porridge, pear, walnuts | Shepherd's pie (leftover) | Niku-dofu, beef, tofu, potato (cooks 2) | Boiled egg with soy; apple oatmeal |
| 4 | Oat porridge | Niku-dofu (leftover) | Chicken, white bean and fennel braise (cooks 2) | Baked egg custard; miso soup |
| 5 | Oat porridge | Braise (leftover) | Miso-simmered mackerel, soft rice | Baked egg custard; yogurt, ginger apple |
| 6 | Oat porridge | Oyakodon | Haddock chowder (cooks 2) | Edamame; miso soup |
| 7 | Soft eggs, tomato white beans | Chowder (leftover) | Sardine, tomato, spinach pasta | Baked sweet potato; edamame |

Fatty fish: salmon (day 1), canned salmon in the okayu (day 2), mackerel (day 5), sardines (day 7). Fatty fish is always cooked fresh.

Day 3 and day 5 breakfasts were not swapped as the critic suggested, because eggs on day 3 pushed saturated fat over 10% at Light; instead the repeat flavour base was removed by moving the second tomato-bean breakfast to day 6 (day 5 is porridge, so day 5 lunch no longer repeats breakfast).

## Sizes

- **Standard** about 2000, covers 1800-2000, 2000-2200, 2200-2400. All pass.
- **Light** 1700, covers 1600-1800. Drops the second snack and scales down the chicken, rice and bread. The 1400-1600 band is dropped: days 1 and 3 could not size within 5% of 1400, and protein per meal, calcium, vitamin E and omega-6:3 failed on several days there.
- No Big.

## Notes

- Dairy is kept on purpose: it carries calcium (1,200 mg target) and the Light days sit just above the 80% line.
- Safety: egg dishes to 74 C, fish 70 C, poultry 74 C, ground meat and beef 71 C; pin bones pulled from mackerel; cold tofu eaten the day the pack is opened; opened canned salmon kept covered and used within 2 days; leftovers into a shallow container within 2 hours (rice or grains within 1 hour), up to 3 days, reheat once to 74 C. No shirataki.

- Iron cap (30 mg a day, new healthy-aging gate): the iron drivers were calcium-set tofu (about 5 mg per 100 g), red lentils and spinach. Changes: no tofu snack, no tofu in the okayu, niku-dofu tofu cut from 170 g to 90 g (beef up to 110 g), smaller spinach portions in the niku-dofu and mackerel, lighter lentils in the soup and pie, tofu in the miso soup cut to 40 g, and snacks moved to lower-iron ones. Calcium now comes mostly from milk, yogurt, canned salmon and sardines with bones, and a little tofu. The chilled-tofu snack is gone, so the cold-tofu handling line went with it. Chowder uses olive oil, not butter, to keep day 6 saturated fat under 10%.

## Final review fixes

- Day 6 breakfast is now porridge (it had put six eggs and the most sodium on one day), so breakfasts run porridge on days 3 to 6 and eggs and beans on days 1 and 7.
- Chowder gets 4 g of butter back (olive oil 3 g), to keep the Maritime flavour now that day 6 has room.
- Niku-dofu is fridge-only (frozen tofu turns spongy); the open tofu block is stored in fresh water for up to 3 days.
- Plan text: calcium-pill line added, Light sentence now lists what really shrinks, freezing line lists only the soup, pie and braise.
- Text tidies: storage line without the rice-and-grains clause where there is none, scrambled eggs step reordered, softer wording on two recipes, finely chopped walnuts, longer edamame simmer, more water for the okayu.
