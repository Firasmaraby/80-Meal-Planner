# South Indian & Sri Lankan Kitchen: draft week (names and one-line ideas)

Written before any grams. Revised after each lab run (see the notes at the bottom).

Places: Tamil Nadu (sambar, thinai upma, bottle gourd kootu with carrot poriyal, Chettinad pepper chicken), Kerala (prawn roast with beans thoran, sardine curry with cabbage thoran, fish moilee with spinach thoran), Karnataka (bisi bele bath, chitranna-style lemon millet, moong kosambari), Andhra (pesarattu), Sri Lanka (egg hoppers with pol sambol, parippu with a kale sambol, mackerel ambul thiyal with a cabbage and carrot mallung).

Carbs: red rice, brown rice and foxtail millet (ragi is not in the food library). No white rice, no wheat. So the week is "whole grains too", with millet on three days.

Fish: Tuesday (sardines), Thursday (Atlantic mackerel), Sunday (Spanish mackerel standing in for seer fish). Never back to back, never a leftover.

## Breakfasts (3 recipes)
- Pesarattu (green moong dosa) with allam pachadi (ginger chutney) and curd, no egg. Mon, Thu, Sun. Soak the moong the night before, no fermenting.
- Foxtail millet vegetable upma with egg podimas (onion and curry leaf scrambled eggs) and curd. Tue, Fri. Urad dal and mustard tempering.
- Egg hoppers with pol sambol (soaked brown rice batter, no yeast). Wed, Sat, the slower weekend breakfast.

## Dinners and the lunches they make
1. Mon: Vegetable sambar with brown rice (drumstick, pumpkin, spinach). 2 servings, makes Tuesday's lunch.
2. Tue: Kerala sardine curry with cabbage thoran and red rice. 1 serving.
3. Wed: Sri Lankan red lentil dhal with a kale mallung and red rice. 2 servings, makes Thursday's lunch.
4. Thu: Mackerel ambul thiyal-style sour pepper fish with leek mallung and brown rice. 1 serving.
5. Fri: Bisi bele bath-style lentil rice with pumpkin and spinach, curd on the side. 2 servings, makes Saturday's lunch.
6. Sat (weekend project): Chettinad pepper chicken (curd marinade) with foxtail millet and a cucumber and onion side. 2 servings, makes Sunday's lunch.
7. Sun: Kerala fish moilee with spinach thoran and red rice. 1 serving.

## Lunches that are cooked fresh
- Mon: Kerala prawn roast with green bean thoran and red rice.
- Wed: Lemon foxtail millet with peanuts and chickpeas, with a cucumber pachadi.
- Fri: Moong dal and cabbage kootu with carrot poriyal and brown rice.
- Tue, Thu, Sat, Sun: leftovers.

## Snacks (2 a day, Light drops the second, Big adds a third)
Boiled egg with lunu miris and Kerala pepper egg fry (the protein on the pesarattu days), badam lassi, guava with chili and almonds, papaya with almonds, chickpea sundal, moong kosambari, spiced neer mor, pepper rasam. Big adds peanut sundal, papaya, steamed sweet potato and more of the above. Light keeps both snacks on days 1, 4 and 7 and drops the second snack on the other days.

## Check against GUIDE section 11
- Cuisine fit: everything is something a South Indian or Sri Lankan home cook cooks. Peanuts, almonds, coconut, sesame oil and tamarind are all native to the kitchen. No flax, no chia, no walnuts, no quinoa.
- Honest names: "style" or "inspired" where I borrowed. Ambul thiyal is made with tuna and goraka in Sri Lanka, so ours is mackerel and tamarind. Moilee uses Spanish mackerel for seer fish (the same family). Gotu kola in the sambol becomes kale. Sambar powder and bisi bele bath powder are both "curry powder" in the food library.
- Three breakfasts are three different plates: a lentil dosa, a millet porridge-like upma, a rice-flour pancake with eggs.
- One flavour base a day: ginger and moong (pesarattu), tamarind and sambar powder (sambar), tamarind and chili (sardine), coconut and turmeric (dhal), pepper and tamarind (ambul thiyal), black pepper and fennel (Chettinad), coconut milk and green chili (moilee).
- Vegetables vary: pumpkin, drumstick, spinach, cabbage, bottle gourd, kale, green beans, carrot, cucumber, tomato. Carrot is the most common (upma, kootu, bisi bele bath, mallung, kosambari) because it carries the vitamin A.
- Raw weights for fish, chicken, dals, rice, millet and eggs. Canned chickpeas only. Shrimp for the prawn roast.
- Every side (thoran, poriyal, mallung, sambol, pachadi, chutney) is written into the recipe that uses it.

## After the first lab runs
- Saturated fat was the big problem: coconut at 10 to 25 g a dish put days at 12 to 14 percent. Coconut is now 3 to 6 g a dish (a spoonful for flavour), coconut milk 8 to 15 g, and canola does the frying. Coconut oil is only a tip.
- Eggs moved to every breakfast (two a morning) because choline for men needs about 440 mg and the rest of a South Indian day is plant-based. Curry leaf, chili powder and sambar powder all bring vitamin E too.
- Seasoning had to shrink: the engine scales salt and ground spices up to 1.8 times at Big, so salt sits near 1.2 g and ground spices plus curry leaf stay under about 4.4 g a serving. Dried red chili, tamarind, ginger and green chili carry the heat and sourness instead.
- Calcium comes from curd in four dishes, neer mor, badam milk and a curd marinade for the Chettinad chicken. Vitamin E comes from canola, eggs and a few almonds (badam milk, guava or banana with almonds).
- Kale shows up in the dhal sambol only, cabbage in two thorans/mallungs, spinach in sambar, bisi bele bath and the moilee thoran. The kootu uses bottle gourd.

## After the chef critic
- Eggs now start four mornings (upma and hoppers). Pesarattu has no egg, and a boiled egg snack on days 1, 4 and 7 carries the choline.
- Carrot is only in the Friday kootu poriyal. Upma uses green beans, kosambari uses cucumber, bisi bele bath uses pumpkin and spinach, and the ambul thiyal side is a leek mallung.
- Hoppers ferment overnight with a spoon of cooked rice. Pol sambol has 12 g of coconut. The dhal side is a wilted kale mallung.
- Sambar, bisi bele bath and the rasam use the real spice blends. The Big size scales ground spices by about 2.3 on the lightest days, so the blends stay at 2 to 3 g a serving.
- Kerala dishes are fried in coconut oil, bisi bele bath in ghee, ambul thiyal in gingelly oil. Chettinad chicken stays in canola because gingelly oil pushed the Saturday omega ratio over 9:1.
- Groceries are 43 at Standard and 44 at Big. One yogurt is used everywhere, jaggery and fresh coriander were left out to stay under 45.

## After the final review
- Hoppers: soak the rice the morning before, blend that evening with cooked rice, curd and only 2 tablespoons of water, ferment overnight.
- Egg snack renamed "Boiled egg with lunu miris" (Sri Lankan relish); pepper egg fry has 1.5 g pepper and turmeric.
- Full spice amounts in Standard: sambar powder 3.5 g, bisi bele bath powder 5 g, Chettinad pepper 2.5 g and coriander 1.8 g. Curry leaf and mustard were trimmed in the bisi bele bath and Chettinad to keep Big under the spice cap.
- Moilee has 30 g coconut milk. Thorans crush the coconut with aromatics. Mortar steps for the chutney and kootu paste. Weekday tips fixed.
