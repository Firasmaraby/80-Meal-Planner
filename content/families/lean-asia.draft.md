# lean-asia draft (dish names, final)

Breakfasts (3 rotating): Greek yogurt bowl with mango, lime, mint and toasted seeds (Mon, Thu); egg and tofu scramble with shiitake, spinach and sweet potato (Tue, Sat); warm silken tofu bowl with edamame, ginger-soy and bonito (Wed, Fri, Sun).

Snacks (7 recipes, 1 or 2 a day; pear yogurt moved to Saturday so Friday's breakfast and snack do not repeat the pear): soy-marinated eggs, carrot and daikon sticks with sesame-miso dip, yogurt with Asian pear and black sesame, smashed cucumber, warm edamame with lime and chili, chilled silken tofu, mango with lime, chili and sunflower seeds.

Sodium plan: reduced-sodium soy by the teaspoon, fish sauce 5 g a portion (about 9 g sodium per 100 g in the library, so the biggest single item), miso 4 to 14 g, 30 g of kimchi in the stew, low-sodium chicken broth for the pho, no clams. Small amounts of iodized salt in the pho, stew, larb, cucumber, mackerel and edamame. Day 2 at Standard 2000 is the tight day (2,977 of 3,000 mg), so seasoning comes from less water in the stew, nabe and miso soup, not from more salt. Max day sodium: 2,528 (Light 1400), 2,593 (1600), 2,883 (1800), 2,930 (Standard 1800), 2,977 (2000), 3,104 (2200, ceiling about 3,300).

Omega: canola is the cooking oil, toasted sesame oil finishes the Chinese dishes, and perilla (egoma) oil finishes the Korean and Japanese ones (scramble, tofu bowl, stew, nabe dip, chilled tofu). Salmon and mackerel three times, edamame most days. Sunflower seeds in three small places (yogurt bowl, mango snack, tofu bowl) for vitamin E. Weekly omega 2.6 to 3.2:1, worst day 6.0:1.

GUIDE 11 and 12 checks: fatty fish Mon, Wed, Fri (non-adjacent; Sat lunch is the salmon nabe leftover); eggs in 2 breakfasts, 2 egg snacks and the stew portion (6 slots); dairy only 4 slots; no walnut, flax or chia crutches; protein 30% or more every day at every band edge; fibre 30 g or more; groceries 45; bands claimed 1400-1600, 1600-1800, 1800-2000, 2000-2200. 1200-1400 not claimed: days 1 and 5 cannot be trimmed below about 1,280 and 1,320 kcal and day 2 falls under 30 g of fibre there. Light drops Friday's snack, Saturday's second snack and Sunday's second snack so those days size down to 1,400.

Revision notes: pork nabe became salmon nabe (omega-3 and choline on Saturday); clams dropped for sodium; groceries cut from 72 to 45 by sharing greens (gai lan in three dishes), tofu, mango and pear across the week; critic and final-review text fixes applied.
