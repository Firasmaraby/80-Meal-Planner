# vegan-latin: draft week (Vegan Latin Kitchen)

Style `vegan`, 7 days, Standard 2100 kcal (1800-2400), Light 1700 (1600-1800), Big 3000 (2400-3200).

| Day | Breakfast | Lunch | Dinner | Snacks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Avena with cinnamon, banana, chia and almonds, cooked in soy beverage | Black bean and roasted sweet potato bowl with lime-salted tofu and pepita crema | Soya and mushroom tinga tacos (makes tomorrow's lunch) | Jicama and orange cup with pepitas; tofu al pastor bites with pineapple |

## Check against GUIDE section 11 (critics' catches)
- Not of the cuisine: everything here is Mexican, Peruvian, Colombian, Brazilian or Cuban. Brazil nuts only in the Brazilian farofa and one soy yogurt cup. Chia only where Mexico uses it (chia fresca, avena, licuado). Almonds as almendras con limon y chile, in licuado and avena. Farofa uses toasted masarepa, said so in the tip (manioc flour is the classic).
- Dish names are honest: "tinga" is soya and mushrooms, "feijoada-style", "picadillo" with lentils and soya, "chupe" without egg and cheese.
- Protein crutches: tofu in 8 slots, soya chunks in 8, tempeh in 6, mushrooms in 9, soy beverage daily (B12). Beans, lentils and quinoa carry the rest.
- Same flavour base twice a day: Tuesday has tomato in the arepa pico, the tinga and the chupe, but chile base differs (paprika, chipotle, aji amarillo).
- One vegetable everywhere: avocado kept to small amounts in four recipes, orange in three, tomato is the cuisine.
- Weekday breakfasts: avena 11 minutes, arepas and rancheros have make-ahead tips.
- Batch staples: all leftovers are recipes that make two portions; no loose rice pot (rice is cooked in each recipe).

## Check against GUIDE section 12 (vegan)
- No animal foods: no lard, queso, crema, honey. Soy yogurt is the crema. Banned foods checked by the lab.
- B12 daily from fortified soy beverage (avena, cafe con leche, licuado, chupe). howItWorks says about 2 ug per cup, many Canadian cartons about half, a supplement is the safe bet.
- Protein at least 20% every day at every size edge, a real protein at each main meal.
- Calcium: soy beverage, calcium-set tofu, collards, chia. Iron with vitamin C (tomato, tomatillo, lime, orange).
- Omega: weekly ratio 4.3 to 5.4:1, no day above 9:1. Canola only for cooking, no olive, sunflower or corn oil (olives appear only as the picadillo olives).
- Choline: soy foods, beans, quinoa, oyster mushrooms, cauliflower, no eggs needed.
- Iodine: iodized salt as the salt. Calcium and iron kept under the upper limits at Big (tofu amounts watched).
