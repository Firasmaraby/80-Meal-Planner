# Ramadan Arabian: draft week (names and one-line ideas)

Places: the Gulf (kabsa, shorbat adas, shorbat shaeer, laban), the Levant (kofta and tabbouleh, sambousek, fattoush, eggah, sayadiyah), Egypt (ful, watermelon with white cheese, khoshaf-style fruit). All recipe ids start with `ramadan-arabia-`. Style `ramadan`: no lunch, breakfast shown as Suhoor and dinner as Iftar. Iftar is two dinner entries (dates and soup first, then the plate), 1-2 snacks after.

Carbs: brown basmati, whole-wheat khubz and flour, coarse and fine bulgur, pearl barley, rolled oats, lentils, chickpeas and fava beans. No white rice or white bread. Freekeh is not in the library, so the freekeh-style soup uses coarse bulgur and says so.

Fish (3, never leftovers): Wed salmon, Fri Atlantic mackerel, Sun rainbow trout. Pastry: one baked sambousek, Sat.

## Suhoor (low salt, slow release; three recipes rotate)
- Ful with a boiled egg, tahini, lemon, cucumber and khubz. Mon, Fri, Sun.
- Cardamom oats cooked in milk with walnuts and almonds, a boiled egg and watermelon. Tue, Sat.
- Zucchini and herb eggah with labneh, cucumber and khubz, 2 servings. Wed, then leftover Thu.

## Iftar: dates and water, then soup (dinner entry 1), then the plate (dinner entry 2)
1. Mon: lentil soup (2 servings) | lighter chicken kabsa, brown basmati (2 servings)
2. Tue: leftover soup | leftover kabsa
3. Wed: chicken and cracked wheat soup, freekeh-style (2) | baked salmon with lemon tahini and fattoush
4. Thu: leftover soup | beef kofta with tabbouleh and tahini
5. Fri: harira-style lentil and chickpea soup with egg (2) | baked mackerel with cumin potatoes, chickpeas, red pepper
6. Sat: leftover harira | baked lamb sambousek with fattoush (weekend project)
7. Sun: Saudi-style barley soup with lean beef | baked trout over caramelized onion and cumin brown rice (sayadiyah-inspired, weekend project)

## Snacks (2 a day; Light keeps one; Big adds a third on 4 days)
Minted laban (Mon, Fri); dried apricot and laban cooler, the qamar al-din treat (Mon, once); orange with almonds and mint (Tue, Sun); saffron and cardamom milk with nuts (Tue, Sat); date-sweetened mahalabia, the one small sweet (Wed, Thu); cumin roasted chickpeas (Wed); yogurt with figs and nuts (Thu, Fri); pomegranate yogurt with walnuts (Sat, Sun). Big adds: dates stuffed with walnuts (Mon), watermelon with feta and walnuts (Tue), hummus with pepper and carrot sticks (Wed), khubz with labneh and tomato (Fri).
