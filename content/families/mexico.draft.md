# Mexican Kitchen: draft week (names and one-line ideas)

Written before any grams, revised after the lab and after the chef critic. This is the final week.

Places: Central Mexico (chilaquiles, huevos a la mexicana, tinga from Puebla, caldo de res, nopales), the North (frijoles charros), Oaxaca (tlayuda-inspired tostadas with guajillo beef and quesillo), Yucatán (pollo pibil), the Pacific coast (Guerrero pozole verde, Baja-style salmon tacos, sardine tostadas) and street-snack touches (esquites, jícama with chile and lime, chía fresca, camote).

Carbs: corn tortillas (whole grain), hominy, beans, corn. The week is "whole grains too".

Fish: Baja-style salmon tacos on Tuesday and sardine tostadas on Thursday. Two days apart, both served fresh.

Real chiles: chipotles in adobo (tinga), guajillo (tlayuda beef), ancho (salmon tacos, frijoles charros), achiote paste (pollo pibil), epazote (chilaquiles, pozole, charros), ground oregano and cumin. Snacks use ground guajillo.

## Breakfasts (3 recipes)
- Chilaquiles verdes with baked tortillas, fried eggs, queso fresco and a lime yogurt crema. Mon, Thu, Sun.
- Huevos rancheros on crisped tortillas with salsa ranchera and pinto beans. Tue, Fri.
- Huevos a la mexicana with tomato, onion, jalapeño and nopalitos, with beans and tortillas. Wed, Sat.

(Molletes were dropped: a breakfast without eggs could not carry choline for the men's panel on the days it landed.)

## Dinners and the lunches they make
1. Mon: Pollo en tinga with black beans on warm tortillas (two tortillas at every size). Makes 2, so Tuesday lunch.
2. Tue: Baja-style salmon tacos (two tortillas at every size) with an ancho rub, cabbage and carrot slaw and black beans. 1 serving.
3. Wed: Caldo de res on bone-in shank with chickpeas, chayote, corn and potato. Makes 2, so Thursday lunch.
4. Thu: Sardine tostadas with beans, jícama slaw and salsa. 1 serving.
5. Fri: Pozole verde with chicken, hominy, epazote and pepitas. Makes 2, so Saturday lunch.
6. Sat (weekend project): Pollo pibil with achiote paste, pickled red onion and carrot, and avocado. Makes 2, so Sunday lunch.
7. Sun: Frijoles charros with a little pork, ancho and epazote. 1 serving.

## Fresh lunches
- Mon: Ensalada de nopales with pinto beans, queso fresco and pepitas.
- Wed: Quesadillas de rajas (charred poblano and nopales) with corn, pinto beans and raw tomatillo salsa.
- Fri: Tlayuda-inspired tostadas with black beans, guajillo beef and quesillo.
- Tue, Thu, Sat, Sun: leftovers.

## Snacks (2 a day, Light drops the second)
Papaya and almond licuado; chía fresca with lime (Mon, Fri) and with orange (Sat); yogurt with orange and pepitas (Tue) or almonds (Wed, Sun); almonds with lime and chile (Tue); jícama; papaya; orange with chile; esquites (Big); camote; guacamole with baked tostadas (Big).

## Checks against GUIDE section 11
- Not of the cuisine: chia only appears in chía fresca. Nothing added for a number that a Mexican kitchen would not use.
- Renamed dishes: tlayuda, pollo pibil, tinga, pozole and charros say "inspired" where we changed the cut, the starch or the method.
- Flavour base twice a day: green tomatillo never lands on the same day as the pozole verde, soups never land twice in a day.
- One vegetable everywhere: cabbage in five recipes, nopales in three, avocado trimmed to modest portions, papaya no more than once a day.
- Protein crutches: eggs only at breakfast; cheese in modest amounts; yogurt stands in for crema.
- Lists match steps: raw chicken, beef, pork, salmon, tomatillo and tomato; dried beans cooked ahead with canned equivalents in the steps.
