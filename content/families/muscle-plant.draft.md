# muscle-plant: Build Muscle Plant-based (draft)

Style `muscle-plant`: protein at least 30% of calories from food (no powders), no animal foods, no fish requirement.
Standard about 2400 (2200-2400 and 2400-2800), Big about 3000 (2800-3200). XL dropped: above 3200 kcal, 30% protein from
soy foods pushes iron past 45 mg and calcium past 2,500 mg (GUIDE §12: upper limits at every size).

## Strategy
- Protein density comes from seitan, tofu (plain firm and silken), tempeh and edamame. Lentils and beans stay modest (kind
  to the gut, higher in iron per gram of protein). Silken tofu is blended into oats, smoothies, lassi and pudding.
- Seitan sits in 12 of the 14 mains (the taco bowl and the rigatoni use tempeh). Taking it out of more dinners failed the
  30% protein gate on days 4, 5 and 7, or pushed iron past 40 mg. The plan text tells people to pick a seitan near 450 mg
  sodium per 100 g and says seitan is wheat gluten.
- B12: a glass of fortified soy drink in most breakfasts plus the smoothie, lassi and pudding, at least 230 g a day.
- Omega-3 (ALA): chia, hemp and walnuts in oats, bowls, pudding, smoothie, cup and pasta bake. Canola oil for cooking.
- Tofu advice: buy a regular firm tofu with about 10% of the daily value for calcium per serving; the plan says a calcium-set
  tofu would add more calcium than the week needs. Iodine and vitamin D are low from food on this soy-heavy week, so the text
  asks people to use iodized salt and talk to a care provider (and to mention the soy if on thyroid medicine).
- Iron and calcium: every day at every edge is at or under 40 mg iron and 2,200 mg calcium (peaks 39.9 and 2,151 at Big
  3200). Tofu is plain firm tofu and the plan does not rely on calcium sulfate. TVP and cocoa are out, spices trimmed.
- Safety: silken tofu dishes keep 2 days, pudding is made fresh each time, cooked pasta, rice and chili cool within 1
  hour, frozen portions thaw overnight in the fridge, roasted soybeans are ready-to-eat soy nuts.

## Checks against GUIDE §11 and §12
- Cuisines: India (dal, curry, chaat, lassi), East Asia (stir-fry, mapo, seitan bites, chilled tofu), Mexico (bowl, scramble,
  chili, guac), the Mediterranean (Greek seitan, rigatoni, silken tofu dip).
- Fruit: blueberry in 3 recipes, mango in 2. No flax anywhere savoury; chia and hemp only in sweet bowls, smoothie and pudding.
- Beans: rinsed no-salt-added cans, 35 to 70 g a meal at Standard, with tofu, tempeh and seitan on the days between.
- Big day 1 adds sticky tofu bites (less silken tofu on a day that already has an oat jar). Big day 5 adds tempeh strips, a non-legume snack, instead of a fourth legume source.
