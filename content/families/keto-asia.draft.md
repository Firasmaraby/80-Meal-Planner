# keto-asia draft: Keto Asian

Japan, Korea, Thailand and China, with no rice, noodles or sugar. Fat comes from fish, sesame, tofu, walnuts, pumpkin seeds, avocado and canola oil, with a little light coconut in two dishes. Protein is lean on purpose (chicken breast, pork tenderloin, lean sirloin, sea bass, clams), because eggs, tofu, salmon, mackerel and sesame are already 10% to 20% saturated. Shrimp is out (the library's raw shrimp is about 570 mg of sodium per 100 g), so is pork belly, and the sugar in teriyaki, bulgogi and Korean banchan is simply left out.

## Deviations and why

- **Big 2800-3200 is dropped.** The sizer scales oil about 1.8 times, and tofu, nuts and seasonings follow while vegetables stay fixed. At 3200 the oil passed 27 g, raw meat reached 410 g, calcium passed 2,500 mg and net carbs passed 40 g. At 2800 it passes with the Big snacks.
- **Written Standard averages about 1,800 kcal**, not 2,100 (days 1,615 to 1,962, lowest Sunday). Oil is held at about 13 g a recipe so Big stays under 27 g, calcium-set tofu is capped by the 2,500 mg limit, and net carbs cap the vegetables.
- **Critic fixes applied:** scramble without walnuts and with more tofu and perilla; krapow without tofu and with double garlic and chili; curry thickened with mashed cauliflower and less water; sundubu with more clams and less water; mapo on doubanjiang with less water; chicken with more ginger, scallion and a splash of water; gyeran-jjim without the seared mushrooms, with avocado and more kimchi; tom yum with more aromatics; hot pot with a stronger broth and a walnut and sesame dip; bulgogi with garlic in marinade and ssamjang; edamame snack dropped for the doubanjiang; marinated egg re-seasoned; avocado relabelled; tips for perilla oil and sea bass.
- **Gate adjustments:** krapow oil is 14 g (not the 16 g asked) and the scramble keeps 90 g tofu, because more oil or tofu breaks the Big oil cap and calcium limit. Sundubu keeps firm tofu because soft tofu would be a 46th grocery.

## GUIDE section 11 check

- **Eggs:** breakfast has an egg on 2 of 7 days (gyeran-jjim), plus the clam stew and two marinated egg snacks. The egg snack on Thursday carries the choline that day, and the sundubu clams carry B12 on Monday, when there is no fish.
- **Dairy:** none at all.
- **Nuts and seeds:** walnuts in 6 of 35 slots (walnut chicken x2, walnut spinach with mackerel, five-spice walnuts x2, hot pot dip) where the cuisine uses them: Chinese walnut chicken, Japanese kurumi-ae, Chinese spiced walnuts. Pumpkin seeds in 2 snacks. Sesame is native to every country in the week.
- **One vegetable everywhere:** spinach is in 6 recipes and bok choy in 5, because they carry the folate, calcium and vitamin A. King oyster mushrooms are in 6 recipes and shiitake in 4, cucumber in 1, avocado in 3 snacks, cauliflower in 2.
- **Same flavour base twice a day:** the clam stew (doenjang, gochugaru) is on Monday with the Thai curry, never with another Korean dish; Saturday's bulgogi wraps follow a Japanese breakfast.
- **Raw weights:** all meat and fish are raw. Clams are shucked and raw, edamame is raw (shelled), the marinated egg is boiled in the steps.
- **Batch staples with no recipe:** none. The marinated egg tip says to make a batch of four and gives the keeping time.
- **Findable:** gai lan, king oyster mushrooms, perilla oil, doenjang and gochugaru come from an Asian grocery. Everything else is in a normal supermarket.

## GUIDE section 12 check (Keto)

- No grains, bread, rice, noodles, potato, sugar, mirin or honey. Tamari and ginger replace the sweet sauces.
- Net carbs: 28 to 30 g at Light, 33 to 34.5 g at Standard, up to 36.2 g at Big 2400 and 38.7 g at Big 2800.
- Saturated fat: worst day 8.7% at Light, 9.1% to 9.4% at Standard, 9.7% at Big.
- Omega-6:3: weekly 4.1 to 4.3 at Light, 4.5 to 4.7 at Standard, 4.8 to 5.3 at Big; worst day 6.3:1 at Standard and 8.1:1 at Big 2400 (Wednesday).
- Fibre and micronutrients: every board nutrient reaches 80% every day. Vitamin D, iodine and biotin are the usual hard ones. The lowest of the rest is fibre (81% on one Standard 2400 day), then vitamin E at Light 1400 (82%) and choline at Light 1600 (82%).
- Sodium: the highest day is about 2,055 mg at Light, 2,780 mg at Standard 2400 and 3,010 mg at Big 2800. Soy, miso, kimchi and fish sauce are all measured in grams.
- Oil: 13 g or less written per recipe, so no recipe passes 27 g at Big.
- Groceries: 44 at Light, 45 at Standard and Big. Dried spices and salt are on the pantry shelf (6).

## Final review fixes (applied)

- Summary and `howItWorks` now name avocado and nuts as the fat, say "fatty fish three times" with sea bass as the lean fish, and no longer call mushrooms greens.
- Gluten: doubanjiang, gochujang and doenjang usually contain wheat, so the plan is not gluten-free. The mapo tip and the last `howItWorks` paragraph say so and point to gluten-free versions.
- Mapo garlic 4 g (some kept for the spinach). Tom yum uses shiitake, so king oyster is not in three dishes on Friday. Salmon tray has a pinch of salt and a smaller miso soup. Mackerel salt is 1.2 g.
- Sundubu and gyeran-jjim egg notes and steps read correctly at 2 and 4 eggs. Gyeran-jjim has a microwave weekday shortcut.
- Text fixes: krapow time, hot pot broth (400 ml) and dip wording, mushroom snack oil and inspired-by, hiyayakko drizzle and perilla tip, marinated egg tip and name, curry water, avocado notes.
