## Checks against GUIDE 11 and 12
- Fatty fish: fresh on days 1 (salmon), 4 (saba) and 7 (salmon), never back to back. Canned mackerel snacks on days 2, 3, 5 and 6 (4 slots) carry the soy-heavy days under 9:1; both are canned Atlantic mackerel, not king.
- Eggs: breakfast on 4 of 7 days (dan bing x2, congee x2); the tray has natto and tofu. Egg whites carry some of it to keep phosphorus down at XL.
- Dairy: yogurt in 2 slots only. No cheese.
- Sodium: reduced-sodium soy in teaspoons, small miso, fish sauce and gochujang; ginger, garlic, scallion, chili, rice vinegar and lime do the work. Nuoc cham fish sauce 3.5 g and saba salt 0.5 g; miso, soy, gochujang and congee salt trimmed. Standard 2200 days 4 and 7 sit within about 45 mg of the ceiling, so nothing may add salt.
- Margins at XL 3700: phosphorus 97% of the upper limit (about 3,875 mg), iron 91%, selenium 90%, zinc 89%. Protein 30.2% at XL 3700 and 30.0% at Big 3200, both day 1.
- Swaps made after review: day 2 and day 6 snacks (yakitori and mackerel onigiri), day 4 and day 7 snacks (hiyayakko and edamame), daikon in place of cucumber in the dan bing and yakitori.
- Proteins vary: chicken, shrimp, tofu, beef, mackerel, pork, salmon, cod, eggs, natto, yogurt.
- Rice: polished (unenriched) white for the Japanese and Korean dishes, brown for stir-fries, basil and salmon, to hold glycemic load and fibre without pushing iron and phosphorus.
- Groceries: 45. Bands: Standard 2200-2400 and 2400-2800, Big 2800-3200, XL 3200-3700. XL 3700-4200 not claimed.
