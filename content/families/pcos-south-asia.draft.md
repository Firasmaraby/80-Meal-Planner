# PCOS-friendly South Asian: draft week

Places: Punjab (chilla, rajma, chicken tikka, missi roti, keema matar), Gujarat (sprouted moong usal, jowar roti), Tamil Nadu (millet pongal, sambar, pepper mackerel with cabbage poriyal), Pakistan (egg bhurji, Lahori trout fry, dum biryani, chaas, fruit chaat).

Shape: protein at every breakfast (paneer chilla, egg bhurji, moong pongal with curd), three meals and one or two snacks, one slower-digesting starch per meal (roti, jowar, foxtail millet, brown basmati), dal, beans or chickpeas most days, vegetables and raita at every dinner. No white rice, naan, maida, jaggery or juice. Carb style: whole grains too.

Fish: Tue salmon, Fri mackerel, Sun trout (three fatty fish, never back to back, always fresh). Weekend dish: Saturday chicken dum biryani with brown basmati, cauliflower, peas and almonds.

Dinners that make lunch: Mon tikka (Tue), Wed keema (Thu), Thu sambar (Fri), Sat biryani (Sun).

Snacks: almonds, chaas, fruit chaat with yogurt, roasted chickpeas, sprout chaat, egg chaat with tomato and onion.

Bands: Standard claims 1800-2000 and 2000-2200. Light claims 1600-1800 only. 1400-1600 is dropped on purpose: days 2, 4, 5 and 6 cannot be trimmed below about 1480 to 1580 kcal without calcium and fibre falling under the line and the glycaemic load going over.

Written Standard days run about 1,840 to 2,030 kcal. Light is the Standard week with the second snack dropped and resized to 1,600, so the sizer trims oil, ghee, paneer and dal first and the grain stays about the same (half a cup of cooked rice or millet); the plan text says so.

Safety (review pass): chicken to 74 C, fish to 70 C, leftovers chilled within 1 hour in a shallow dish and reheated once to 74 C, rice dishes eaten the next day, marinade for the cauliflower set aside before raw chicken goes in, sprouts at a rolling boil until soft, iodized salt, no alcohol, no herbal teas, no supplements, no claims.

Final review pass: Light line corrected, produce and board washing line added, kingfish (surmai, vanjaram) mercury guard on the mackerel, keema thaw and reheat line, one-ball jowar roti for the usal and one ball per portion for the keema, lemon and mint wording matched to the steps, sprout chaat text matched to the 5-minute boil, ration-style rice wording removed, chaat masala added to the keema list, and the egg snack is now a masala egg chaat so cucumber appears at most twice a day.
