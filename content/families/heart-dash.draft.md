# DASH North American: draft week (final)

Places: Canada (apple-sage pork with red cabbage, split pea soup, maple), the US (turkey chili, sheet-pan chicken, Sunday roast, avocado toast, diner-style scramble), the American South (Louisiana red beans and rice, almond-crusted trout), the Southwest (black bean and egg bowl, salmon tacos, guacamole).

Fish: Mon seared salmon, Wed almond-mustard trout, Sat salmon tacos. Never back to back, never a leftover.
Rice: Monday's chili rice feeds Thursday's bowl, Thursday's pork rice feeds Friday's red beans.
Salt: only a pinch in six dishes (chili, sheet-pan chicken, pork, red beans, split pea soup, roast chicken). Everything else is lime, lemon, cider vinegar, garlic, onion, ancho, smoked paprika, herbs and roasting.
No cured or deli meat, no cheese. No-salt-added beans and tomatoes, low-sodium broth, reduced-sodium bread, unsalted nuts.

Breakfasts (3): blueberry banana overnight oats with walnuts (Mon, Wed, Sat); veggie scramble with toast and orange (Tue, Fri, Sun); blueberry and toasted oat parfait with almonds (Thu). Fruit at every one.

1. Mon: L seared salmon and white bean salad with a boiled egg. D turkey and two-bean chili, makes 2.
2. Tue: L leftover chili. D sheet-pan lemon-herb chicken with potatoes and broccoli, makes 2.
3. Wed: L leftover chicken. D almond-mustard trout (mustard and almond crust, trout amandine style) with sweet potato and garlicky spinach.
4. Thu: L Southwest black bean and egg bowl. D apple-sage pork with brown rice and braised red cabbage, makes 2.
5. Fri: L leftover pork. D Louisiana red beans and brown rice with turkey and spinach, makes 2.
6. Sat: L leftover red beans. D chili-lime salmon tacos with red cabbage slaw (fish taco night).
7. Sun: L Quebec-style split pea soup with garlic croutons. D Sunday roast chicken thighs with root vegetables and a Dijon pan sauce (weekend dish; Dijon, sage, thyme and smoked paprika rub, so it differs from Tuesday's lemon-rosemary tray; vegetables roasted first, chicken on top).

Snacks (2 a day, Light drops the second): Mon orange with almonds + avocado toast; Tue warm nutmeg milk + apple with maple-ginger yogurt; Wed peppery egg with red pepper strips + apple with maple-ginger yogurt; Thu warm nutmeg milk + white bean dip with carrots and celery; Fri banana yogurt with walnuts + guacamole with baked chips; Sat peppery egg + white bean dip; Sun banana yogurt with walnuts + guacamole. Big adds no snacks: the sizer grows the dishes instead (a third snack would force repeats past two a week).

Crutch check (GUIDE 11):
- Eggs: one every day (7 slots): scramble Tue, Fri, Sun; boiled egg in Monday's salad (choline fails without it); fried egg on Thursday's bowl; boiled egg snack Wed and Sat. Choline at 1,800 kcal needs about an egg a day and the week has no other dense source (fish, chicken, turkey and pork help). Each egg is part of the dish.
- Dairy: 17 slots, never more than 3 in a day. Avocado: 5 slots (Mon toast, Thu bowl, Fri and Sun guacamole, Sat tacos). DASH asks for low-fat dairy daily and calcium is the gate that decides it. Most are small: yogurt in dressings and a swirl, milk in oats and a splash in the eggs.
- Carrot in 4 recipes (sheet pan, soup, roast, bean dip), red pepper in 4, smoked paprika in 4 (chili, red beans, tacos, soup), blueberries at 4 breakfasts. Cinnamon never at breakfast and snack on the same day; orange never twice a day; avocado never twice a day.
- Walnuts and almonds sit in oats, parfait, a nut crust on trout, a pork pilaf and nut snacks, not sprinkled on savoury dishes for numbers.

Light claims 1600-1800 only (as heart-med did). I tested adding 1400-1600: only Saturday and Sunday fail, because they can't be trimmed below about 1500 and 1600 kcal (sized to 1509 and 1606 at the 1400 edge), so it isn't claimed.
Beans note: the library has no no-salt-added kidney, white or black bean entries (only reduced-sodium black beans and chickpeas), so the boiled entries stand in for no-salt-added canned beans, and the notes say so.
