## GUIDE section 11 and 12 check

- Cuisine fit: ugali, sukuma wiki and pilau are Kenyan home food; polenta, pasta with sardines and tuna and beans are
  Italian (the ragu takes collards, the sardine spaghetti is a tomato and breadcrumb dish, not pasta con le sarde); flax porridge (Red River style), maple and baked beans and a mustard roast are Canadian; carrot rice, salsa
  verde, tostadas and arroz con leche are Mexican; egg rice with miso and a teriyaki-style salmon bowl are Japanese.
  Flax only where Canadian porridge uses it. Corn tortillas are the only tortilla.
- Grocery cap: about 45 distinct groceries, so the pantry is shared across the five cuisines (one oil each for the pan
  and the Italian dishes, one citrus each for lemon and lime, shared tomato, spinach, carrot, onion, tuna and beans).
  Fresh oregano and thyme were swapped for dried basil and dried thyme so the herbs sit on the spice shelf.
- Vitamin E comes from almond butter (rice cakes, bites), olive and canola oil, spinach and flax; choline from eggs,
  beef, chicken and fish. Omega: flax, canola, salmon and sardines carry it (weekly about 3.5 to 4:1, no day above 7.6:1).
- No sports drinks, gels or powders in any recipe; the text mentions them for sessions over about 90 minutes.
- Salt: miso, soy (reduced sodium), tuna, sardines and salted potatoes stay inside the sodium ceiling at every band.
- Food handling: 74 °C poultry, 71 °C ground meat, 70 °C fish, cooled rice within 1 hour, leftovers in shallow
  containers within 2 hours, washing hands and boards after raw meat and fish.
- Review fixes: arroz con leche is made fresh and the second bowl kept 2 days; ugali, polenta, pasta and rice are
  chilled within 1 hour; potatoes travel with an ice pack; produce washing, opened tuna, ragu freezing and thawing,
  turkey reheated covered with a splash of water, huevos well per egg, tostadas as chips at XL, spinach cut from the
  ragu (collards) and carrot cut from the beans on toast.
- Repeats: every recipe at most twice outside breakfasts; no banana twice in a day; the almond butter is never in two
  slots on the same day; eggs at six meals (4 breakfasts, 2 sandwiches).
