# North African Spice Road: draft week (names and one-line ideas)

Written before any grams, then revised after the lab. This is the final week.

Places: Morocco (chicken and apricot tagine, harira, chermoula sardines, lamb and prune tagine, bissara, msemen, carrot and orange salad, sardine kefta with zaalouk), Tunisia (slata mechouia with tuna and egg, harissa and caraway mackerel with a diced Tunisian salad), Algeria (chakchouka, couscous with beef and vegetables, tajine zitoune with chicken and olives).

Carbs: chickpeas, lentils, fava, barley, potato, oats, whole-wheat khobz (pita), whole-wheat flour and whole-wheat couscous. No white rice, white pasta or refined couscous, so this is a "whole grains too" week.

Fat: olive oil only. No canola, flax or chia. Walnuts and almonds only where Maghrebi cooks use them: tagines, sweet porridge, dates and walnuts, dates in warm milk.

Fish: Wednesday (fresh chermoula sardines) and Friday (fresh Tunisian harissa mackerel), two days apart and never leftovers. Tinned mackerel (Monday lunch) and tinned sardines (Tuesday snack, Saturday lunch) back them up, because every non-fish day still has to stay under 9:1 omega-6 to omega-3 with an olive oil kitchen.

## Breakfasts (4 recipes)
- Chakchouka with green pepper, harissa, caraway and two eggs, with khobz. Mon, Sun.
- Cinnamon oat porridge with dates, orange, almonds, walnuts and a little orange blossom water. Tue, Fri.
- Bissara from dried fava beans, a pot of two cooked Tuesday evening while the harira simmers. Wed, with the second portion on Thu.
- Whole-wheat msemen-style flatbread with fried eggs, olives and tomato. Sat, the slow weekend breakfast.

## Dinners and the lunches they make
1. Mon: Moroccan chicken tagine with apricots, almonds, chickpeas and pumpkin. 2 servings, makes Tuesday's lunch.
2. Tue: Harira with lentils, chickpeas, tomato and celery, thickened with beaten egg. 2 servings, makes Wednesday's lunch.
3. Wed: Chermoula sardines roasted over potatoes and carrots. 1 serving.
4. Thu: Algerian-style couscous with beef, chickpeas, carrot and zucchini, with a lben-style yogurt drink. 2 servings, makes Friday's lunch.
5. Fri: Tunisian harissa mackerel with a barley and chickpea salad and a diced Tunisian salad. 1 serving.
6. Sat (weekend project): Lamb and prune tagine with toasted almonds and a cucumber and carrot salad. 2 servings, makes Sunday's lunch.
7. Sun: Algerian tajine zitoune, chicken with green olives, preserved lemon, potato and chard. 1 serving.

## Lunches
- Mon: Moroccan carrot and orange salad with tinned mackerel mashed on khobz.
- Thu: Slata mechouia with tuna, egg and olives.
- Sat: Sardine kefta simmered in warm zaalouk, in khobz.
- Tue, Wed, Fri, Sun: leftovers.

## Snacks (1 or 2 a day, each recipe at most twice)
Plain yogurt with honey and roasted chickpeas (Mon); sardines on khobz with tomato and harissa and yogurt with apricots (Tue); yogurt with dates and dried apricots with almonds (Wed); dates and walnuts in warm milk and orange with cinnamon and orange blossom (Thu); a boiled egg with cumin (Fri, one snack); orange slices and almond-stuffed dates (Sat); dates and walnuts in warm milk and yogurt with apricots (Sun). Big adds one extra snack a day. Light (1600 to 1800 only) drops the second snack on every day except Friday.

## Check against GUIDE section 11 (after the chef critic)
- Not of the cuisine: no chia, flax or canola. Walnuts sit with the dates and in the porridge, almonds sit in the tagines.
- Renamed dishes: the msemen is "inspired" (whole-wheat, quick fold). The harira is vegetarian and thickened with beaten egg. The slata mechouia says what it is.
- Fish: Monday lunch (tinned mackerel on khobz), Tuesday snack (sardine sandwich), Wednesday dinner (fresh sardines), Thursday lunch (tuna, not fatty), Friday dinner (fresh mackerel), Saturday lunch (sardine kefta). The olive-oil kitchen needs it to keep every non-fish day under 9:1.
- Protein crutches: eggs only where the dish has them (chakchouka, msemen plate, slata mechouia, harira) and one boiled-egg street snack that the choline gate asked for on Friday. Yogurt is a snack, plus a lben-style drink with couscous.
- Flavour base: harissa is in the chakchouka, but there is no chakchouka on Friday, when the harissa mackerel is the red dinner.
- One vegetable everywhere: carrot, pumpkin, zucchini, eggplant, chard, celery, cucumber and tomato are spread out. Chickpeas are in 5 recipes (tagine, harira, couscous, Friday's barley salad, one roasted snack).
- Counts: dairy 10 slots, eggs 7 slots, nuts 10 slots, fatty fish 5 slots plus tuna.
- Missing from the library: freekeh. Whole-wheat couscous, dried fava, tuna in oil and orange blossom water are now real entries and are used.
