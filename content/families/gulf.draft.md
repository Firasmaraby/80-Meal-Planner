# Gulf Kitchen: draft week (names and one-line ideas)

Places: Emirates (balaleet, saloona, harees, tamr w laban), Kuwait (machboos, marag bamia, robyan), Saudi (foul with khubz, harees), Oman (sardines, kanaad), Qatar and Bahrain (harees, shrimp rice, dakkous). All recipe ids start with `gulf-`.

Carbs: brown basmati, whole-wheat khubz, whole-wheat thin spaghetti for the balaleet, cracked wheat (coarse bulgur) for the harees, chickpeas, lentils and fava beans. No white rice or white bread, so the week reads "whole grains too". Freekeh is not in the library and wheat berries are read as refined by the carb-style check, so nothing here pretends to be freekeh.

Fish: Tuesday (grilled kanaad, Spanish mackerel, with dakkous) and Friday (roasted sardines, cumin sweet potatoes, cucumber and radish salata). Plus shrimp on Sunday. Never a leftover.

## Breakfasts (3 recipes, never back to back)
- Gulf foul with one boiled egg, tahini, lemon, cumin and khubz. Tue, Fri, Sun.
- Balaleet-inspired saffron vermicelli (whole wheat) with a cardamom omelette and toasted almonds. Mon, Thu.
- Beid wa tamatem, eggs with tomato and green chili, with cucumber and khubz. Wed, Sat.

## Dinners and the lunches they make
1. Mon: Chicken machboos with loomi, brown basmati, almonds and raisins. 2 servings, makes Tuesday's lunch.
2. Tue: Grilled kanaad with dakkous, a cucumber and carrot salad and khubz. 1 serving.
3. Wed: Lamb saloona with loomi, eggplant and pumpkin and khubz. 2 servings, makes Thursday's lunch.
4. Thu: Beef and okra marag with loomi, chickpeas, over a little brown basmati. 2 servings, makes Friday's lunch.
5. Fri: Roasted sardines with cumin sweet potatoes and a cucumber and radish salata. 1 serving.
6. Sat (weekend project): Lamb harees with ghee and cinnamon, laban and a cucumber, mint and parsley salad. 2 servings, makes Sunday's lunch.
7. Sun: Shrimp with tamarind and tomato over brown basmati, with carrot salad. 1 serving.

## Lunches
- Mon: Gulf lentil soup (shorbat adas) with lemon and khubz.
- Wed: Nakhi-style chickpeas over labneh with radish, cucumber and herbs, with khubz.
- Sat: Gulf-style chicken shawarma plate with garlic labneh sauce and salata.
- Tue, Thu, Fri, Sun: leftovers.

## Snacks (2 a day, Light drops the second)
Dates with cold laban, cucumber and mint laban, saffron and cardamom milk with a date and almonds, orange with pistachios, pomegranate with pistachios and mint, dates stuffed with almonds, dates with pistachios, roasted chickpeas. Big adds a third on five days: dates with pistachios, khubz with tahini and date (twice), almonds, pistachios and raisins, pomegranate.

## Check against GUIDE section 11 (after the chef critic)
- Dairy: 10 slots a week, at most 3 in a day. Labneh only in the nakhi plate and the shawarma sauce. No labneh on eggs, soup or fruit.
- Eggs: every breakfast has some egg, but foul carries one egg (not two). An egg-free foul failed choline on days 2 and 5.
- Stews: saloona keeps baharat, turmeric, pumpkin and a whole chili; marag keeps coriander seed, lemon and cilantro. Loomi and tomato paste are in machboos, saloona and marag.
- Day 5 tomato overload fixed: the sardine salata is cucumber and radish.
- Honest names: "balaleet-inspired", "Gulf-style" shawarma and foul, "inspired by robyan", "nakhi" credited as the street-side chickpeas.
- Not applied: the optional cardamom and vinegar in the shawarma marinade (the recipe is at the 14 ingredient cap), and a different second cucumber laban (accepted twice).
- Rose water and date syrup are tips only, to protect the 45 grocery limit.
