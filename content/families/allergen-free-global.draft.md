# Five-Allergen-free Kitchen: draft week (names and one-line ideas)

Home cooking made without peanuts and tree nuts, eggs, dairy, shellfish and sesame. Wheat, soy, fish and mustard are used on purpose, and the plan text says so. Regions: Canada, Mexico, the Mediterranean, Japan. This is the final week, after the lab and the critic's review.

Carbs: oats, brown rice, potato, rye bread, corn tortillas, hominy, whole-wheat pasta. "Whole grains too".

Fish: Monday canned salmon sandwich, Wednesday fresh maple-mustard salmon, Friday canned sardine pasta, Saturday canned salmon cakes. Four fish meals in different formats, and the fresh salmon is never a leftover.

## Hard nutrients without eggs and dairy
- **Choline** shapes the week. Chicken breast, pork tenderloin, beef, salmon, canned salmon, sardines, calcium-set tofu and edamame (the prepared, shelled kind, which carries a choline value) turn up in most meals, and toasted wheat germ goes into the oats. Light holds 1400, 1600 and 1800 only because protein stays high (about 30% of calories). It is a protein-forward, phosphorus-rich week and the plan text says it does not suit a kidney diet.
- **Calcium** comes from fortified soy drink, calcium-set tofu, canned salmon and sardines with their soft bones, kale, broccoli, white beans and soy yogurt. The notes say what to look for on the label (tofu about 45% of the daily value in an 85 g serving, soy drink about 25 to 30% per cup).
- **Vitamin D and B12** come from salmon, canned salmon, sardines and the fortified soy drink. Vitamin D and iodine are "hard from food" and stay low without dairy and eggs; howItWorks says the salt is iodized and to ask about a supplement.
- **Omega 6:3**: no walnuts or chia (nuts are out). Pre-ground flax goes into the porridge, overnight oats, smoothie and yogurt snack so every day has some, and canola is the oil in the Mexican, Japanese and Canadian dishes. Olive oil stays in the Greek stew and the Sicilian-style pasta.

## Breakfasts (3 recipes)
- Cinnamon apple oat porridge with ground flax, wheat germ and sunflower seed butter. Mon, Thu, Sun.
- Tofu scramble a la mexicana with black beans, warm corn tortillas and avocado. Tue, Fri.
- Blueberry overnight oats with soy yogurt, flax, wheat germ, sunflower seed butter and pumpkin seeds (made the night before). Wed, Sat.

## Dinners and the lunches they make
1. Mon (Mexico): chicken in red pumpkin seed sauce (pipián rojo) with rice, black beans, spinach and avocado. Makes 2, so Tuesday lunch.
2. Tue (Greece): pork and white bean stew with kale, lemon and dill, rye on the side. Makes 2, so Wednesday lunch.
3. Wed (Canada): maple-mustard salmon with roasted potatoes, carrots and broccoli. 1 serving.
4. Thu (Canada): beef, potato and kale soup with carrot, simmered until tender (about 1.5 hours, with a pressure-cooker tip). Makes 2, so Friday lunch.
5. Fri (Sicily): sardine pasta with broccoli, tomato, lemon and toasted rye breadcrumbs. 1 serving.
6. Sat (Mexico, the weekend project): pork pozole rojo with hominy, raw spinach ribbons, avocado, lime and baked tortilla strips (the broth simmers 20 minutes before the lean pork goes in for 20 more, so it stays tender). Makes 2, so Sunday lunch.
7. Sun (Japan): Japanese-style tofu and vegetable curry with brown rice, thickened with grated potato and apple (no boxed roux). Makes 1.

## Snacks (2 a day, Light drops the second; the first of each day is the one that carries nutrients)
Warm edamame with lemon and chili, blueberry soy yogurt with flax, apple with a sunflower seed butter dip, blueberry-spinach-flax soy smoothie, baked tofu bites with lime-soy dip, cucumber with lime, chili and pumpkin seeds, smoky roasted white beans, no-bake oat and sunflower seed butter bites, lime-paprika toasted pumpkin seeds, and the week's treat, baked apple with a maple oat crumble (Saturday). Big adds one a day: rye toast with seed butter and blueberries (Monday and Saturday), oat bites, blueberry soy yogurt, black bean salsa with baked chips (twice), baked apple with maple oat crumble.

## Hidden allergens (every ingredient with a "may contain" or hidden-ingredient risk carries a note)
Rye bread and the toast (milk, egg, sesame; buy packaged, not from a bakery counter), whole-wheat pasta (egg), chicken broth (milk), curry powder (sesame, mustard, fenugreek), Dijon, reduced-sodium soy sauce (sesame), corn tortillas (milk, egg), oats and wheat germ (nuts, sesame; no bulk bins), ground flax (buy pre-ground, check the label), sunflower seed butter, pumpkin seeds, fortified soy drink, soy yogurt, calcium-set tofu, prepared edamame, canned salmon and sardines. Dried chiles (peanuts and sesame packed on shared lines), dried spices and blends are covered in howItWorks and the chile notes. The three seed snacks and the overnight oats tell cooks to chop or grind pumpkin seeds for children under 4. howItWorks adds the allergist line and the seed caution. Left out on purpose: mayo, pesto, chocolate, granola and cereal bars, tahini and hummus, sesame oil, furikake, coconut, oyster sauce, fresh and filled pasta.

## Home safety
howItWorks has a home cross-contact paragraph (cook these recipes first, wash hands with soap and water because sanitiser gel does not remove allergens, clean boards, knives, pans and counters, fresh spoons in the seed butter and soy yogurt, separate toaster or toaster bags). Recipes toast bread in the oven or a dry pan. Opened tofu and opened tins go in the fridge in a covered container and are used within 2 days. The salmon sandwich filling stays cold.

## Checks against GUIDE section 11 and 12
- Not of the cuisine: flax is the only "numbers" seed, and it is a Canadian staple. Wheat germ goes in oats and the smoothie only. No chia or walnuts.
- Renamed dishes: pipián rojo, pozole rojo, kare raisu and pasta con le sarde say "inspired" where the dish departs from the original (grated potato and apple for the roux, no pine nuts, lean pork in the pozole).
- Same flavour base twice in a day: apple and cinnamon sit at breakfast on porridge days only (the apple snacks are on non-porridge days, with one Big exception, Sunday's baked apple).
- One vegetable everywhere: kale only in the stew and the soup (four days), spinach in six recipes, carrot in seven, cucumber in four. Black beans never appear three times on one day.
- Protein crutches: tofu is the protein of the scramble, the bowl, the curry and the bites, never sprinkled on top. Soy yogurt is a dressing or a snack.
- Lists match steps: raw meat and fish, and brown rice boiled in plenty of water and drained; canned fish, beans and tomatoes; edamame is the pre-cooked, shelled kind and the steps only heat it.
- Binder without egg: potato and oats in the salmon cakes.
- Sizes written near their label: see the lab (Standard averages about 2,100 kcal as written).
- Groceries: 45 distinct (the cap), with the dried chiles counted.
