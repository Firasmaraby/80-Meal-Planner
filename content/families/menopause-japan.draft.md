# Menopause Japanese: draft week (final review applied)

Soy-rich, real washoku: ichiju-sansai shapes, grilled and simmered fish, mugi-gohan (brown rice cooked with pearled barley), small miso soups. No seaweed (iodine upper limit) and no kombu. No mirin or sake; honey sweetens.

Breakfasts (3): natto and tamagoyaki bowl with tofu miso soup (Mon, Wed, Fri); egg, tofu and shirasu okayu (Tue, Thu, Sun); kinako walnut oatmeal with mikan (Sat).

1. Mon: L canned salmon and edamame bowl. D tofu and chicken hambagu, makes 2.
2. Tue: L leftover hambagu. D grilled salmon, spinach goma-ae, daikon miso soup.
3. Wed: L hiyashi soba with tofu, egg and edamame. D chikuzen-ni, makes 2.
4. Thu: L leftover chikuzen-ni. D mackerel simmered in miso with spinach.
5. Fri: L shrimp and egg donburi. D beef and tofu sukiyaki nabe, makes 2.
6. Sat: L leftover sukiyaki. D pork and tofu gyoza with kabocha (weekend project), makes 2.
7. Sun: L leftover gyoza. D salmon chanchan-yaki.

Fatty fish: Mon (canned salmon), Tue, Thu, Sun dinners, always fresh.
Snacks: persimmon or mikan with almonds, yogurt with kinako and almonds, almond kinako soy milk (almonds are a modern snack for vitamin E), steamed kabocha, soy-marinated egg (Tue and Sun), edamame (plain and with sesame), kinako soy milk. Light drops the second snack.
Calcium: two tofus (about 683 and 171 mg per 100 g) are balanced so no day at any edge passes 1,770 mg; the plan note carries the supplement advice. Egoma oil and walnuts keep omega-6:3 under 9:1 on fish-free days.
Safety: 74 C poultry and reheating, 71 C pork, 70 C fish; rice cooled in 1 hour, 2 to 3 days in the fridge, freeze for days 4 to 7; frozen edamame boiled; produce washed.
Claims: Light 1600-1800 only (1400-1600 failed).
Final review: warfarin line says to skip natto; hand and board washing added; gyoza steps work at Light (leftover filling becomes patties); Sunday has one tofu dish fewer; iodine stated honestly as low; soy line for hormone-sensitive cancer in howItWorks.
