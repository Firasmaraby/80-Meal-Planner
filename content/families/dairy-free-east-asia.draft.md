# Dairy-free East Asian: draft week (after critic round)

Places: China (savoury soy milk, jiachang doufu, jiaozi), Japan (miso set, teriyaki salmon, nabe), Korea (mackerel jorim, sundubu, iriko bokkeum), Vietnam (sardines in tomato, lemongrass rice plate).

Breakfasts: savoury soy milk bowl with an egg, edamame and walnuts (Mon, Wed, Fri), miso soup set (Tue, Thu), salmon congee with egg ribbons (Sat, leftover Sun).
Dinners: Mon braised pork and tofu, Tue teriyaki salmon, Wed lemongrass chicken plate, Thu braised mackerel, Fri sundubu, Sat jiaozi (weekend project), Sun chicken nabe. Mon, Wed, Thu, Fri and Sat dinners make the next lunch.
Fresh lunches: Mon sardines in tomato, Wed cold sesame udon with canned sardines.
Fish: sardines Mon and Wed (iriko Tue, Fri), salmon Tue, Sat and Sun, mackerel Thu and Fri.

Calcium honesty: tofu is counted at the library's firm (150 mg per 100 g) and soft (111 mg) values and the text says plain firm or soft tofu, with a label check in howItWorks. Tofu was cut to a few dozen grams a day, chilled tofu snacks removed, and the dou jiang bowl has no tofu. With firm tofu counted at 683 mg per 100 g and soft tofu at 200 mg, no day at any edge passes 2,500 mg (peak 2,398). As counted, no day passes 1,971 mg. Sundubu soft tofu restored to 120 g; Big has no egg snacks; the soy milk bowl has one egg.
Bands: Light claims 1600-1800 only. 1400-1600 was dropped because Sunday would not size below about 1,550 kcal without losing a nutrient tile. Standard and Big claim all their bands.
Rice: three batches (Sunday, Wednesday, Friday), quantities in howItWorks.
Seaweed: a pinch of nori only. Groceries: 45 (lab max).

Final round: hand washing moved straight after raw meat and fish, rice cups corrected (2, 1, 1 dry), no calcium supplement line, braise renamed as a pork dish, carrot out of the udon, soy egg snack removed.
