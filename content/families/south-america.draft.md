# South American Table: draft week (names and one-line ideas)

Written before any grams, then revised after the lab. This is the final week.

Places: Colombia (huevos pericos, arepa de choclo, ajiaco, chocolate caliente), Brazil (cuscuz de milho com ovo, feijoada, moqueca, açaí, mamão), Peru (cooked-fish ceviche with camote and toasted corn, solterito, lomo saltado, sardines with yuca and salsa criolla, quinua con leche, pepas de zapallo, cancha), Chile (salmon with pebre and potatoes, inspired by salmon a lo pobre) and Argentina (lentil guiso, chimichurri chicken).

Carbs: whole-grain cornmeal, corn, quinoa, black beans, lentils, potatoes, yuca, sweet potato and a little brown rice. No white rice and no refined flour, so the week is "whole grains too". Masarepa is not used at all: the arepas are arepas de choclo, blended from fresh corn and whole-grain cornmeal. No refined tapioca.

Fats: a neutral oil for everyday cooking, olive oil in the Chilean and Argentine dishes (pebre, chimichurri, guiso) and the solterito. No flax or chia.

Fish: salmon on Tuesday, sardines on Thursday, mackerel moqueca on Saturday (leftover Sunday lunch). Cooked cod ceviche on Monday lunch is the lean white-fish dish.

## Breakfasts (3 recipes)
- Huevos pericos (Colombian scrambled eggs with tomato and scallion) with an arepa de choclo and hot chocolate. Mon, Thu, Sun.
- Cuscuz de milho with a fried egg, grilled queijo coalho, tomato and papaya (Brazilian Northeast, a tapioca-free alternative to pão de queijo). Wed, Fri.
- Quinua con leche: quinoa porridge with evaporated milk, a little cocoa and banana. Tue, Sat.

## Dinners and the lunches they make
1. Mon: Feijoada made light: black beans, lean pork, a little chorizo, garlic collards, orange and vinagrete, with a little brown rice. Makes 2, Tuesday lunch.
2. Tue: Baked salmon with pebre, potatoes and garlicky acelga (chard) (inspired by salmon a lo pobre). 1 serving.
3. Wed: Argentine lentil guiso with a little chorizo, collards, potato and pepper. Makes 2, Thursday lunch.
4. Thu: Sardines with boiled yuca, salsa criolla and avocado. 1 serving.
5. Fri: Lomo saltado with roasted potatoes, peppers, tomato and a little brown rice. Makes 2, Saturday lunch.
6. Sat (weekend project): Moqueca de cavala, a mackerel stew with tomato, peppers, a little coconut milk and colorau (urucum), with garlicky collards and brown rice. Makes 2, Sunday lunch.
7. Sun: Ajiaco-inspired chicken and potato soup with guascas, corn, avocado and lime. 1 serving.

## Fresh lunches
- Mon: Ceviche-style cooked cod with camote and corn.
- Wed: Pollo a la parrilla with chimichurri, roasted sweet potato and ensalada criolla.
- Fri: Quinoa solterito with black beans, corn and queijo coalho.
- Tue, Thu, Sat, Sun: leftovers.

## Snacks (2 a day, Light keeps only the first)
Pepas de zapallo tostadas, mamão with lime, toasted corn, naranja with cinnamon, maní with lime and ají, açaí na tigela (snack only, with peanuts), banana with peanuts, camote asado (with and without pepitas). Big adds one a day: choclo con queijo coalho (twice), arepita de choclo (twice), pepas, naranja, toasted corn.

## Checks against GUIDE section 11
- Not of the cuisine: no chia, no flax, no walnuts or almonds. Peanuts and pumpkin seeds appear only as the Peruvian and Brazilian snacks they are. Every vegetable and herb is one a South American cook uses.
- Renamed dishes: feijoada, salmon a lo pobre, lomo saltado, ajiaco, moqueca, solterito, ceviche and the quinoa porridge all say "inspired" in `inspiredBy` where we changed the cut, the starch or the method.
- Flavour base twice a day: tomato and onion are the cuisine's base, but each day gets a different preparation (hogao-style eggs, pebre, salsa criolla, vinagrete, chimichurri, sofrito); papaya and orange never double up on the same day.
- One vegetable everywhere: collards in three recipes (feijoada, guiso, moqueca), chard in the salmon, avocado in five (small amounts), tomato in about nine (it is the base of the cuisine); bell pepper, corn and potato are spread across different days.
- Protein crutches: eggs only at five breakfasts; queijo coalho in modest amounts; no yogurt crutch; no nuts in savoury dishes.
- Lists match steps: raw pork, beef, chicken, salmon, cod and mackerel; dry beans, lentils, quinoa and cornmeal.
- Batch staples: the Sunday bean pot and the rice are both given amounts and keeping times in `howItWorks`.
