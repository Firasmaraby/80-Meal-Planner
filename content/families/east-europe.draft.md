# Eastern European & Georgian Table: draft week (names and one-line ideas)

Written before any grams, then revised after the lab. This is the final week.

Places: Ukraine (borscht with beans, kasha with mushrooms, syrniki), Poland (gołąbki, mizeria, śledź on rye, sauerkraut, tvorog snacks, liver pâté), Georgia (lobio, satsivi, pkhali, khmeli-suneli, adjika), the Balkans (pasulj with sweet peppers and smoked paprika), the Baltics and Carpathians (baked trout with dill potatoes and kefir).

Carbs: buckwheat, barley, rye bread, whole-wheat lavash, white and red beans, potatoes, oats. No white rice, white bread or pasta, so this is a "whole grains too" week because of the rye and lavash.

Fat: rapeseed (canola) oil for cooking, a little butter, and unrefined sunflower oil only as a finishing drizzle (kasha, the sauerkraut salad). Walnuts only where Georgians use them (lobio, satsivi, pkhali) and in a baked apple for Big. Sunflower seeds (semechki) and poppy seeds (mak) are the vitamin E and calcium carriers. No flax or chia.

Fish: Monday (pickled herring on rye), Wednesday and Friday (smoked sprats on rye), Sunday (baked trout). No two of the main fish meals are back to back.

## Breakfasts (3 recipes)
- Kefir with soaked buckwheat, apple and raspberries. Mon, Thu, Fri. Stirred together the night before.
- Kasha with mushrooms and a fried egg. Tue, Wed. About 20 minutes, with a tip to cook the kasha the night before.
- Baked tvorog and oat syrniki with raspberries. Sat, with the second portion warmed on Sun.

## Dinners and the lunches they make
1. Mon: Ukrainian borscht with white beans and beef, half a boiled egg and a little sour cream. 2 servings, makes Tuesday's lunch.
2. Tue: Georgian lobio with walnuts and herbs, sulguni laid over the hot beans and lavash. 2 servings, makes Wednesday's lunch.
3. Wed: Gołąbki, cabbage rolls with barley and pork in tomato sauce. 2 servings, makes Thursday's lunch.
4. Thu: Chicken with kasha and mushrooms, with mizeria. 2 servings, makes Friday's lunch.
5. Fri: Balkan pasulj, white beans and sweet pepper stew with beef. 2 servings, makes Saturday's lunch.
6. Sat (weekend project): Satsivi, chicken in walnut sauce, with a cucumber and herb salad. 2 servings, makes Sunday's lunch.
7. Sun: Baked trout with dill potatoes, kefir and a sauerkraut and carrot salad. 1 serving.

## Standalone lunch
- Mon: Open rye sandwiches with pickled herring, onion and dill, with sauerkraut and a pickle.

## Snacks (one a day; Big adds one)
Tvorog with dill and cucumber on rye (Mon), tvorog with apple and poppy seeds (Tue), sardines on rye (Wed, Fri), chicken liver pâté on rye (Thu), spinach pkhali with lavash (Sun). Big adds an apple with sunflower seeds, a kefir with raspberries, a baked apple with walnuts and a second tvorog or pkhali.

## Check against GUIDE section 11
- Not of the cuisine: no chia, flax. Walnuts only in Georgian dishes. Mushrooms only in the kasha dishes. Poppy seeds are a Polish and Ukrainian staple.
- Renamed dishes: gołąbki with barley is "inspired" (the old village filling), the pasulj is "inspired" (canned beans), satsivi is "inspired" (thighs, lighter sauce), syrniki are baked and oat-based so "inspired", the sardines stand in for sprats and say so.
- Protein crutches and eggs: eggs are in the Tue and Wed kasha breakfasts, the syrniki (one egg binds two portions), half an egg in the borscht (Ukrainian habit), half an egg garnishing the satsivi and one egg hidden in the kasha (the old fluffy-kasha trick). No egg snacks. The choline gate (men's 440 mg line) is what forces the eggs and the one liver snack; 10 egg slots in all.
- Dairy: kefir, tvorog, sour cream and cheese are the everyday food of this table, 15 slots counting small dollops, never more than 3 in a day.
- Nuts: walnuts in lobio (Tue, Wed lunch), satsivi (Sat, Sun lunch) and pkhali (Sun). 5 slots, 2 on Sunday only (satsivi leftover and pkhali).
- Flavour base twice in a day: Thursday has kefir and buckwheat at breakfast and kasha at dinner (flagged: buckwheat appears twice, but one is soaked and cold, the other toasted and savoury). Tuesday has beans in the borscht and in the lobio (white beans with beet and cabbage, then red beans with walnut).
- One vegetable everywhere: apple is in 5 recipes but never twice in a day at Standard. Cucumber is in 3 recipes. Dill is the cuisine's own herb, in most.
- Liver: one small portion (about 45 g) on Thursday only, well under the 3,000 µg preformed vitamin A limit even at Big.
- Missing from the library: matsoni (kefir), utskho suneli (blue fenugreek, mentioned in the satsivi tip), kvass, tkemali, shoti and mchadi (lavash). Sulguni, smoked sprats and dried porcini now exist and are used; ajvar exists but is not used (grocery cap).

## Revisions after the lab
- First pass had 59 groceries and failed protein, calcium, choline and vitamin E on several days. Fixes: low-fat kefir everywhere, tomato paste instead of crushed tomatoes, no pumpkin seeds, pear, plum, blueberries, parsley, chives, radish, horseradish, honey, red onion or yogurt, one berry (raspberries).
- Calcium: sardine bones, mozzarella in the lobio, kefir on the potatoes and in the mizeria, poppy seeds in three recipes.
- Vitamin E: toasted sunflower seeds on the kasha, in the kefir bowl and the snacks, and a drizzle of unrefined sunflower oil.
- Choline and B12: half eggs in the borscht and satsivi, an egg in the chicken-kasha, beef in the pasulj, and one liver pâté.
- Light drops no snack: the engine trims each day to 1400 without losing a nutrient.

## Revisions after the chef critic
- Chicken kasha water measured for both portions, satsivi egg garnish removed, borscht names rye and drops potato to stay at 14 ingredients, Saturday snack is a baked apple with poppy seeds, lobio uses sulguni laid over the hot beans, pasulj renamed a Balkan bean and pepper stew and simmered longer, gołąbki filling moistened and baked 60 minutes, kefir bowl uses the kasha buckwheat, liver pâté in butter with a little rapeseed oil, syrniki use two eggs for two portions, smoked sprats replace sardines, dried porcini replaces some cremini, adjika dropped from the lobio to keep 45 groceries.
