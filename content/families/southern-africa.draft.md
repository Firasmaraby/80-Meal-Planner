## GUIDE section 11 check

- Not of the cuisine: peanuts go into the Zimbabwean greens (dovi is the real thing). Pumpkin seeds and sunflower
  seeds are everyday in South Africa. Almonds are in the Cape oats, the mabele topping and one snack, nowhere
  savoury. No walnuts, chia or flax. Apricots are Cape staples. Liver (beef in the Zimbabwean stew, chicken in the
  peri-peri tray) is eaten across the region.
- Renamed dishes: "bobotie-inspired" (lentils share the work, brown rice for the yellow rice), "boerewors-style
  patties" (lean pork with the coriander, clove and nutmeg of the sausage), "snoek-style" (Atlantic mackerel stands in
  for snoek), "Cape Malay-style" curry (curry powder and tamarind stand in for the blend), umngqusho made with dried
  hominy because samp is not on the shelf, collards for rape greens and morogo.
- Protein crutches: eggs only where they belong (chakalaka eggs twice, the bobotie custard), no egg on a salad.
  Biltong is a snack, never a meal.
- Same base twice in a day: chakalaka eggs on Tue and Sat, chakalaka and beans on Thu and Fri, so Friday breakfast is
  oats and Thursday breakfast is mabele.
- One vegetable everywhere: butternut in the Tue curry and the Sat salad, never on the same day. Collards in 4
  recipes, with spinach kept for the quick dishes. Guava in 6 slots across different forms.
- Weird breakfasts: porridge, oats and eggs with beans and toast are all real South African breakfasts.
- Batch staples: mabele says soak overnight, cook for two mornings. Rice for the bobotie is cooked for two portions.
  The umngqusho pot says how long it keeps and that it freezes.
- Slot counts (Standard): dairy 10 (milk or kefir, butter in 4 more slots in small amounts), egg 4 (2 breakfasts, the
  bobotie custard at dinner and its leftover lunch), nut or seed 13, fatty fish 5 slots (4 distinct meals, one reheated).

## Revision after the chef critic

- Beef liver out of the Zimbabwean stew (chicken livers stay on the Saturday peri-peri tray). Collards out of the
  eggs and the fish curry, spinach in. Mabele topped with peanuts and sunflower seeds, not almonds. Oats are now
  "South African", the biltong snack has almonds, and the guava lemon chili snack is gone.
- Curated staples used: Cape Malay curry masala, chakalaka spice, boerewors spice and biltong. Samp and sugar beans
  stay as hominy and pinto beans, named honestly.
