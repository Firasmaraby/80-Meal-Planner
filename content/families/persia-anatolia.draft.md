# Persian & Turkish Table: draft week (names and one-line ideas)

Written before any grams, then revised after the lab. This is the final week.

Places: Iran (ghormeh sabzi from Tehran, fesenjan from Gilan, kuku sabzi, nan-o-panir-o-sabzi, a sabzi polo-style salmon), Turkey (menemen, Antalya piyaz, mercimek corbasi, Adana-style kofte with coban salatasi, Southeast Anatolian lahmacun, Black Sea sardines with karalahana kale, kuru fasulye, uskumru).

Carbs: brown basmati (Persian dishes), bulgur (Turkish pilav), whole-wheat flatbread, white beans, kidney beans, lentils, chickpeas, potato, a little cornmeal. No white rice or white bread, so this is a "whole grains too" week.

Fat: olive oil only. No canola, flax or chia. Walnuts only where Persian and Turkish cooks use them: nan-o-panir, fesenjan, and the dates and fig snacks. Hazelnuts are the Black Sea nut: in the sardine crust and the fig, date and orange snacks.

Fish: Wednesday (cornmeal sardines), Friday (salmon with herb rice), Sunday (uskumru). Two days apart, never leftovers.

## Breakfasts (3 recipes)
- Nan-o-panir-o-sabzi: flatbread, feta, walnuts, a soft-boiled egg and a plate of herbs and cucumber. Mon, Wed, Fri. 10 minutes.
- Menemen with a little white cheese. Tue, Thu. 15 minutes.
- Kuku sabzi, a herb frittata with barberries and yogurt, the slower weekend breakfast. Sat, with the second portion cold on Sunday.

## Dinners and the lunches they make
1. Mon: Ghormeh sabzi (beef, kidney beans, herbs, loomi) with brown basmati. 2 servings, makes Tuesday's lunch.
2. Tue: Chicken kofte with bulgur pilav and coban salatasi. 2 servings, makes Wednesday's lunch.
3. Wed: Cornmeal and hazelnut sardines with Black Sea kale and lemon potatoes. 1 serving.
4. Thu: Etli kuru fasulye (white beans, a little beef, kale) with bulgur pilav. 2 servings, makes Friday's lunch.
5. Fri: Saffron salmon with herb rice and grilled tomatoes. 1 serving.
6. Sat (weekend project): Fesenjan-inspired chicken with walnut and pomegranate, brown basmati. 2 servings, makes Sunday's lunch.
7. Sun: Grilled mackerel with tahini sauce, sumac onions and arugula. 1 serving.

## Standalone lunches
- Mon: Antalya-style piyaz, white beans with tahini and a boiled egg.
- Thu: Mercimek corbasi, red lentil soup with an arugula salad.
- Sat: Lahmacun-style flatbread with ayran.

## Snacks (1 or 2 a day; Big adds one, Light drops about one)
Roasted chickpeas, figs with walnuts and hazelnuts, pomegranate with walnuts, yogurt with honey and figs, orange with hazelnuts, pomegranate with mint, dates with walnuts and hazelnuts, orange with mint, tahini and honey on flatbread. Big adds ayran, mast-o-khiar and more fig and date plates.

## Check against GUIDE section 11
- Not of the cuisine: no chia, flax or canola. Pomegranate molasses is in fesenjan only. Rose water is not used.
- Renamed dishes: the kofte is chicken ("inspired by Adana"), the lahmacun is "style" on a flatbread, the salmon rice is "inspired by sabzi polo ba mahi", the sardines are "inspired by hamsi tava", the fesenjan is "inspired".
- Eggs: 7 breakfast slots plus the piyaz lunch. The choline gate forces an egg on the nan-o-panir mornings (the Persian plate often comes with one) and the Monday piyaz. No egg snacks.
- Dairy: 10 slots at Standard (feta 5, yogurt 5), never more than 2 in a day. Big adds more.
- Nuts: 10 slots at Standard, never more than 2 in a day. Walnuts carry the omega-3 on the days without fish.
- Flavour base twice in a day: Thursday has menemen and kuru fasulye, so the bean stew has no green pepper and the soup has only a spoon of paste. Monday has a herb plate at breakfast and ghormeh at dinner, so the breakfast herbs are served raw.
- One vegetable everywhere: tomato, cucumber and parsley are the cuisine's own. Cucumber is in 2 recipes at Standard, tomato in 6, parsley in 11 (a herb by the handful, as intended).
- Missing from the library: kashk, salca (tomato paste stands in), fresh tarragon (dried used), dried mulberries, lavash and sangak (whole-wheat pita stands in), sivri pepper (green bell pepper), pekmez (honey).

- Staples: lavash replaces pita, biber salcasi replaces tomato paste, pekmez replaces honey.
