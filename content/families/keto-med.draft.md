# keto-med draft: Keto Mediterranean (revised after the chef critic)

Fat comes from olive oil, olives, avocado, hazelnuts, almonds, walnuts, chia and fish. Butter, cream and bacon never appear. There is no cheese, and Greek yogurt is the only dairy. Olive oil is about 14% saturated, so the extra fat is roughly half oil and half nuts and avocado, and oil per dish is capped around 8 to 11 g so Big never passes the 27 g limit.

## Critic fixes and where I deviated

- Breakfast grid: the critic and coordinator asked for chia on days 1 and 6 only, yogurt on 2, 3 and 5, omelette on 4 and 7. I tried it (later, day 3 became the omelette). Yogurt on day 5 put that day at 10.4% saturated fat, and the omelette on day 7 put it at 10.4 to 11.2%, so the lab failed. The working grid is chia on days 1, 5, 6 and 7 (puddings are the lowest-saturated-fat breakfast), yogurt on 2, omelette on 3 and 4. The yogurt bowl no longer has chia; it has ground flax for fibre instead.
- Oil: the critic's oil amounts (up to 24 g a dish) push Big to 30 to 35 g of oil per serving. Oil is 8 to 13 g per recipe instead, and the calories come from nuts, avocado, olives and fish.
- Olives: capped at about 90 g a day, rinsed, and removed from the avocado snack. Capers rinsed. Shop tips for tuna and shrimp added.
- Fried almonds with pimenton replace the day 7 poached-shrimp snack. The cold chicken bites are gone. Tuna cucumber boats and the avocado and cucumber salad were cut as unused.
- Shrimp salad: tahini became an almond-garlic dressing (frees a grocery, adds flax).
- Cacciatore: chicken trimmed from 210 g to 190 g, with more mushrooms and lower tomato, so no Big serving passes the 400 g raw cap (largest 395 g). Big day 6 gets an avocado-tuna snack add, and the Big adds are marinated olives (day 1), avocado-tuna (days 2 and 6) and tapenade (day 4).
- Choline: the tuna-stuffed eggs now sit on days 5 and 7 and the poached shrimp on days 2 and 3.
