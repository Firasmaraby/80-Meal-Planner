# Heart-healthy Asian: draft week (final)

Places: Japan (cold soba with edamame, miso-glazed mackerel with sesame spinach, sesame oats, kurumi-ae walnut dressing, hiyayakko), China (ginger-scallion salmon, ginger-garlic tofu, shiitake congee, walnut soy milk, garlic-chili tofu bites), Korea (tofu, mushroom and egg stew on a gochujang and gochugaru base), Vietnam (lemongrass shrimp and tofu, chicken and cilantro salad, pork noodle bowl, chicken pho), Thailand (lime-chili steamed trout).

Fish: Tue coho salmon, Thu Pacific mackerel, Sun trout. Never back to back, never a leftover. Plain peeled shrimp Mon.
No cured or pickled-in-brine foods. Reduced-sodium soy sauce in teaspoons, miso 2.5 to 7 g, fish sauce 1.5 to 3 g (pho 2.5, trout 2.5, stew 1.5), no added salt. Low-sodium broth, unsalted peanuts and walnuts, fresh soba and rice vermicelli cooked in unsalted water. Fish sauce was capped by Big 3200 day 7 (3 g in the trout breaks 2,000 mg).

Breakfasts (3): soft egg scramble with shiitake and spinach on toast (Mon, Wed, Sun); toasted sesame oats with soy milk and tangerine (Tue, Thu, Fri); ginger shiitake congee with tofu and peanuts (Sat, slower weekend breakfast).

1. Mon: L cold soba with edamame. D lemongrass shrimp and tofu with broccoli, makes 2.
2. Tue: L leftover shrimp and tofu. D ginger-scallion salmon with bok choy.
3. Wed: L Vietnamese chicken, cabbage and cilantro salad. D ginger-garlic tofu with broccoli, shiitake and carrot, makes 2.
4. Thu: L leftover tofu stir-fry. D miso-ginger mackerel with sesame spinach.
5. Fri: L lemongrass pork noodle bowl. D Korean-style tofu, mushroom and egg stew, makes 2.
6. Sat: L leftover stew. D chicken pho with shiitake and bok choy (weekend project), makes 2.
7. Sun: L leftover pho. D Thai-style steamed trout with carrot and broccoli.

Dinner grains are half brown rice, half pearled barley (mugi-gohan style), which holds glycemic load down and adds fibre.

Snacks (2 a day, Light drops the second):
- Mon: pineapple with toasted walnuts; chili-lime edamame.
- Tue: ginger-soy marinated egg; pineapple with lime, chili and peanuts.
- Wed: walnut soy milk; sesame cucumber and edamame pickle.
- Thu: ginger-soy marinated egg; chili-lime edamame.
- Fri: walnut-miso cucumber and edamame; pineapple with lime, chili and peanuts.
- Sat: walnut soy milk; edamame-ginger dip with carrot sticks.
- Sun: chilled tofu with ginger and scallion; sesame cucumber and edamame pickle.

Big adds one a day: Mon sesame-garlic tofu bites, Tue chilled tofu, Wed pineapple with walnuts, Thu edamame-ginger dip, Fri carrot sticks with peanut-lime dip (carrot only, so cucumber is not in three recipes), Sat walnut-miso cucumber, Sun sesame-garlic tofu bites (garlic and chili so Sunday does not have two ginger-sesame-soy tofu snacks).

Crutch check: eggs in 7 slots (3 scramble breakfasts, 2 marinated-egg snacks, the stew at Fri dinner and Sat lunch), always part of the dish. Tofu in 8 slots, edamame in 7, peanuts as a small garnish in 8, walnuts in 4 (omega-3 on the no-fish days). Tangerine only at breakfasts (6), cucumber 6 slots, carrot 8 slots. No dairy. No red meat (lean pork tenderloin once).

Sodium reality: written Standard days run 1,150 to 1,400 mg, and Big scales them up, so the Big days near 2,000 were trimmed (no cornstarch, no kimchi, fish sauce in splashes, fresh soba because dried soba is salted). Max at Big 3200 is 1,953 mg.

Groceries: 45. Light claims 1600-1800 only, as for the other heart families. Ground cinnamon stays (no stick in the library); mint is only a pho tip because no grocery is free.
