# Heart-healthy Mediterranean: draft week (final)

Places: Greece (lemon chicken, fakes, village salad, gemista, yogurt with honey and walnuts), Italy (tuna and white beans, salmon with kale pasta, sardines with fennel), Spain (espinacas con garbanzos, paprika chickpeas, pan con tomate), the Levant (tabbouleh, sumac trout with tahini, hummus, stuffed dates).

Fish: Tue salmon, Thu sardines, Sun trout. Never back to back, never a leftover. Unsalted tuna at three lunches.
No cured meat, no olives. Feta only a spoonful on Wednesday's salad. Salt only a pinch in three dishes; the rest is lemon, vinegar, garlic, herbs and spice. No-salt-added beans, lentils, tomatoes and tuna.

Breakfasts (3): walnut oats with apple and cinnamon (Mon, Wed); Greek yogurt with orange, tahini-honey and walnuts (Fri); one-egg-and-whites tomato and spinach omelette (Tue, Thu, Sat, Sun).

1. Mon: L tuna and white bean salad with a boiled egg. D lemon-oregano chicken with potatoes, carrots and tomatoes, makes 2.
2. Tue: L leftover chicken. D salmon with whole-wheat pasta, tomato and kale.
3. Wed: L Greek village salad with chickpeas, tuna, egg and a little feta. D fakes, lentil soup, makes 2.
4. Thu: L leftover fakes. D sardines with fennel, tomato and white beans.
5. Fri: L parsley tabbouleh with lentils, tuna and a soft egg. D espinacas con garbanzos with chopped egg, makes 2.
6. Sat: L leftover espinacas. D gemista, peppers stuffed with lean turkey and bulgur (weekend project), makes 2.
7. Sun: L leftover gemista. D sumac trout with tahini sauce, bulgur and lemony kale.

Snacks (2 a day, Light drops the second): yogurt with dates and almonds or walnuts, hummus and crudites, white bean dip, paprika chickpeas, apple and walnuts, tzatziki and carrot, stuffed dates. Big adds white bean dip, pan con tomate (twice), hummus, unsalted-tuna cucumber rounds and orange with walnuts.

Crutch check: an egg appears somewhere every day (9 slots), because choline at 1,800 kcal needs about an egg a day and the library has no other dense source; eggs are always part of the dish (omelette, salad, tabbouleh, espinacas). Dairy is 10 slots, never more than 3 in a day. Walnuts and almonds sit in dishes and snacks the cuisine already uses (Greek yogurt and walnuts, Spanish almonds in the thickener, Levantine stuffed dates). No red meat; lean turkey only in the gemista.

Light claims 1600-1800 only. Written 1400-1600 failed: Monday and Wednesday can't be trimmed below about 1510 and 1560 kcal without dropping calcium, biotin or choline under 80%.
