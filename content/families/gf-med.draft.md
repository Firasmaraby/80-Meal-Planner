# gf-med draft week (Gluten-free Mediterranean)

Breakfasts rotate three: buckwheat porridge with cinnamon pear, walnuts and yogurt (Mon, Wed, Fri), Greek yogurt with orange, walnuts and crunchy buckwheat (Tue, Thu), Spanish tortilla de patatas with spinach (Sat, Sun leftover).

| Day | Lunch | Dinner | Snacks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Salade nicoise (potato, green beans, tuna, egg, olives) | Greek fasolada with spinach and olives (makes 2) | orange and toasted almonds; tzatziki with carrot and cucumber |
| Tue | Fasolada (leftover) | Gemista, rice and beef stuffed peppers and tomatoes, spinach in the filling (makes 2) | boiled egg with olives and tomato; roasted chickpeas |
| Wed | Gemista (leftover) | Roast salmon with lemon-oregano potatoes and shaved fennel (fatty fish) | boiled egg with olives and tomato; tzatziki with carrot and cucumber |

| Fri | Lentejas (leftover) | Socca with roasted mackerel and a tomato, olive and parsley salad (fatty fish) | orange and toasted almonds; tuna and chickpea salad |
| Sat | Soupe au pistou with white beans | Weekend saffron risotto with shrimp, mussels and tomato | tuna and chickpea salad; romesco with cucumber and zucchini |
| Sun | Escalivada with chickpeas and sardines | Soft polenta with chicken and mushroom ragu and garlicky spinach | Greek yogurt, honey and walnuts; olive tapenade with fennel and carrot |

Starch rotation: beans (Mon), rice in gemista (Tue), potatoes (Wed), lentils (Thu), chickpea flour (Fri), arborio risotto (Sat), polenta (Sun). Buckwheat is at breakfast only.

Checks against GUIDE section 11 and 12:
- Fatty fish: salmon Wed, mackerel Fri (not adjacent), plus canned sardines Thu and Sun. Tuna and shellfish are extras.
- Eggs: only 2 breakfasts (tortilla), one in the nicoise, one on the lentejas, 2 snacks. Not every breakfast.
- Dairy: about 11 slots across the week, kept to places the dishes already use it (yogurt, feta, parmesan, milk in porridge). Never 3 in a day.
- Flavour bases: tomato is not at breakfast, and each dinner leads with a different vegetable (spinach, pepper, fennel, carrot, zucchini, mushroom). Cucumber, fennel and carrot are spread across snacks rather than repeated.
- Nuts are used where the cuisine does: walnuts with yogurt and in porridge, almonds with oranges, in romesco and in the gemista (standing in for pine nuts). No chia or flax.
- Hidden gluten: tips flag stock, chorizo, sausages, polenta, cornmeal, chickpea flour, spice blends, paprika, tuna and tapenade, without nagging.
- No wheat, barley, rye, spelt, farro, couscous, orzo, semolina or regular soy sauce. No bread, no pasta. Buckwheat, chickpea flour, cornmeal and rice carry the starch.
