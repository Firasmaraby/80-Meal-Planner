# Vegetarian Mediterranean: draft week (final, after the chef critic)

Regions: Greece (strapatsada, fasolakia, spanakopita and manitaropita, yogurt with walnuts), Italy (pasta e fagioli, frittata, lupini), Spain (lentejas with chard and carrot, garlicky mushrooms, sherry vinegar), the Levant (ful, falafel, tabbouleh, labneh, hummus, dukkah). Shakshuka comes from the Maghreb and the Levant.

Vegetarian: no meat, poultry or fish. Eggs and dairy are welcome, but the week leans on beans, lentils, chickpeas and lupini for protein. B12 and choline have to come from eggs and dairy, so every day has an egg dish, a yogurt dish or both.

## Breakfasts (3 rotate)
- Strapatsada, Greek tomato eggs, on toast (Mon, Fri).
- Thick yogurt with honey, walnuts, figs and a little ground flax (Tue, Thu, Sat).
- Ful medames with a boiled egg, tomato and tahini (Wed, Sun).

## Check against GUIDE 11 and 12
- Eggs: 9 slots at Standard (strapatsada x2, ful x2, frittata x2, shakshuka, pie x2); Big adds 1 more egg snack. B12 and choline force an egg or dairy dish every day.
- Dairy: 14 slots with feta and halloumi, 8 of them yogurt or labneh. Max 3 in a day (Fri).
- Flax: 6 g in the yogurt bowls and 8 g as a falafel binder, the minimum that keeps the omega ratio in range once walnuts alone could not.
- Walnuts in 9 slots (yogurt, pear, figs, cucumber yogurt, lentil salad). No almonds, pistachios or chia.
- Tomato in 7 recipes plus Big's pan con tomate; mushrooms in 4 recipes; cucumber 4; carrot 5.
- Staples used: Kalamata olives, sherry vinegar, za'atar, sumac, Aleppo pepper, labneh, dukkah, pomegranate molasses.
- No fish, anchovies or meat stock.
