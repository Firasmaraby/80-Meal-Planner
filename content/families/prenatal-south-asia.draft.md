## GUIDE section 11 and 12 check

- Gentle breakfasts: poha, cheela and bhurji are mild; no chili in the cheela.
- Cooked-through methods: eggs until firm, goat and chicken to no pink, fish until it flakes, leftovers chilled within 2 hours and reheated until steaming.
- No alcohol, no raw sprouts (chaat bowl uses cooked canned beans), no liver, no deli meats, paneer from pasteurised milk, no green papaya.
- Caffeine: one cup of chai on two days only.
- Iron leans on masoor, moong, goat, sardines, sesame and cashews with lemon, tomato, orange or guava nearby.
- Spinach turns up twice on Tuesday (bhurji, leftover dal) and Sunday (bhurji, rohu jhol); fenugreek leaves are in three dishes only (keema, methi matar paneer, sardine masala).
