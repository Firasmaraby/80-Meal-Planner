# Vegetarian Global Kitchen: draft week (final)

Regions: Mexico (huevos a la mexicana, black bean enchiladas), Japan (tamago and tofu rice bowl, miso aubergine, chilled tofu), Italy (minestrone con riso), India (toor dal tadka, cardamom yogurt, golden milk), Ethiopia (misir wat with greens), the Levant (roast cauliflower and chickpea plate with labneh, za'atar, egg and tomato), Britain (lentil, mushroom and walnut shepherd's pie, beans on toast, porridge).

Vegetarian: no meat, poultry, fish, fish sauce or anchovies (the pie gets its savoury depth from miso, not Worcestershire). Beans, lentils, tofu and chickpeas carry the protein; eggs, milk, yogurt, labneh and cheese bring B12 and choline. Omega-3 is ALA from ground flax and walnuts.

## Breakfasts (3 rotate)
- Apple-cinnamon porridge with walnuts, almonds, ground flax and yogurt (Mon, Sat, Sun).
- Creamy garlic white beans on toast with a poached egg and mushrooms (Tue, Thu).
- Huevos a la mexicana with black beans and warm tortillas (Wed, Fri).
